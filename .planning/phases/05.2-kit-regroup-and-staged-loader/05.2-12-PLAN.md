<objective>
Make the core's pay-in visibly take each step and mark each stage, and show every energy price before use: a 9-pip row and stage beats on the core, and a reusable cost badge on the paying energy objects that reads its number from the config.

Purpose: ENRG-CORE (D-73 "nothing deducts" proved and made readable) and the cost half of ENRG-FEEDBACK (D-74 badges).
Output: core pip row, ring pop and chimes, CostBadge scene, badges on bounce core, charge gate and Loader field, extended core test and badge cases.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

Plan set notes: read "Plan set notes" in `05.2-01-PLAN.md`.

## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **Cost display of the core:** the 9-pip row (three groups of three, lit per paid step) is the core's "clock pips above it"; it gets no separate number badge. The Loader field shows the stage it needs (2), since its cost is paid by the Loader itself.
