**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **Dead profiles are removed, not kept for later:** the snap bloom was rejected by the user, so its profile (ReactionToLoad BITE) goes too (RESEARCH "Cuts and blast radius" recommends removal as the cleaner default). Phase 7 can add a profile again when a level needs one.
- **Enum:** BITE is the last ReactionToLoad value, so removing it shifts nothing; the remaining presets keep their serialized ints.
- The profile and station names are named in the actions because they must be found and removed; no edited file may keep them in a comment.
