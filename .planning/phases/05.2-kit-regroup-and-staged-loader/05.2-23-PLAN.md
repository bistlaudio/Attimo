<objective>
Remove the dead code the cuts of plans 18-22 left behind: the ForceVolume DRAG profile with its move-scale field and the leaf storm's strip look, the player's move-scale request, and the look-less wall surface block with its config, presets and base scene; renumber the force-volume profile enum safely with by-name assertions for every preset.

Purpose: KIT-CUT (the dead-code part of the object cut, D-81 / guardrail 3).
Output: DRAG, the strip look and the move-scale request gone, enum renumbered, presets updated and asserted by name, the wall surface block family deleted and gated.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

Plan set notes: read "Plan set notes" in `05.2-01-PLAN.md` (environment, metrics doc rule).

## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **Enum renumber:** DRAG sits in the middle of the ForceVolume enum; removing it shifts GRAVITY 2 -> 1 and RADIAL 3 -> 2. The only serialized users are gravity_zone.tres and attraction_field.tres (grep at HEAD: no scene inlines a ForceVolumeConfig; the DIRECTIONAL presets stay 0), both updated in the same task and asserted by profile name.
- The two preset ints and the deleted wall-surface presets reach the metrics doc's Kit values with its next regeneration (plan 28).
- **Wall surface block:** after plan 18 no scene uses it (only its base scene, which test_kit_blocks' contract points at) and the player never referenced its class (group names only); it goes here with the other dead code.
- The removed field is named in the action because it must be found and removed; no edited file may keep it in a comment.
