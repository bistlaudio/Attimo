## Notes and decisions per Part

### Part A (formerly 05.2-14): Decisions recorded in Part A (Claude's discretion, CONTEXT)

- **One new profile, no flags (guardrail 1):** `TimedMotionConfig.Profile.PLATES` is appended (index 7, serialized ints stay). Plates reuse the deck machinery (`_build_decks`, AnimatableBody3D, `carrying_platform`, zero deck velocity). Part B adds the one number that distinguishes the light path (`plate_life`).
- **Timing:** next plate every 0.35 s, pitch 3.0 m (run speed 8.5 m/s covers 3 m in 0.35 s).
- The core's level-2 output was the switched speed pad; it becomes the light bridge here. Level 3 still drives the north steampunk drawbridge until Part B gives it the light path.

### Part B (formerly 05.2-15): Decisions recorded in Part B (Claude's discretion, CONTEXT)

- **Light path timing:** next plate every 0.35 s, each lives 1.2 s, pitch 3.0 m (run speed 8.5 m/s covers 3 m in 0.35 s, so running keeps up and stopping drops you). While the receiver stays on, the run repeats with a period of plate_count * plate_interval + plate_life.
- The core's level-3 output was the north steampunk drawbridge; it becomes the light path here, so after Part B the core switches only turquoise objects.

### Part C (formerly 05.2-16): Decisions recorded in Part C (Claude's discretion, CONTEXT)

- **Glider:** a PATH ping-pong deck, SWITCHED. New meaning of SWITCHED for a ping-pong PATH (no existing user): on = runs the route, off = travels back to its first point and rests, so the glider always waits at the gate. Start path_speed 5.0 m/s; the gap is the best stage-3 Loader reach over pitch 0..80 degrees (LoaderMath.shot_reach, about 26 m) plus 1 m, so it cannot be crossed any other way; the builder raises path_speed if the one-way route time would not stay at least 1 s under GATE_HOLD.
- `glider_course` replaces `bridge_course` for the gate station; `bridge_course`, `pad_course` and the steampunk signal objects are removed in Part D at the latest.

### Part D (formerly 05.2-17): Notes

- After plan 06 and Parts A-C nothing energy-driven uses the two scenes any more: the core drives the energy drawbridge, light bridge and light path, the gate the glider, AND / OR energy drawbridges. Only the steampunk mechanism stations, the bridge and pad courses and test cases still reference them.
- test_movement_gym's steampunk profile check counts the drawbridge (HINGE) among the steampunk area's Timed motion profiles; it loses HINGE here (the energy drawbridge lives in the energy area).
- The drawbridge preset `resources/kit/timed_motion/drawbridge.tres` stays (the energy drawbridge uses it).
