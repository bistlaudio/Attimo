<objective>
Turn the two remaining energy movement objects into the user's rules: the gravity zone becomes a strong-downward-gravity obstacle (a dash gets you out), and the attraction field loses its own cost (it costs only through the dash you need to escape).

Purpose: ENRG-RULE (D-72 attraction field, D-77, D-91 gravity accumulator, D-88 users).
Output: request_gravity_boost in the controller, strong gravity zone, free attraction field, tests and coupling updated.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

Plan set notes: read "Plan set notes" in `05.2-01-PLAN.md` (environment, D-88, sign texts, metrics doc rule).

## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **D-91 strong gravity:** a second accumulator `_zone_gravity_high` (max of the tick, clamped 1.0..4.0) next to the existing low one (min, 0.05..1.0); gravity is multiplied by both. Multiplying with the Loader shot phase (0.3x) is intended and predictable.
- The gym signs of the gravity zone and the attraction field are rewritten in plan 13; gravity_zone.tres reaches the metrics doc with its next regeneration (plan set notes).
