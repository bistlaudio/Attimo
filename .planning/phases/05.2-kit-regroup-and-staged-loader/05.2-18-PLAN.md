## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

Plan set notes: read "Plan set notes" in `05.2-01-PLAN.md` (environment, cut gate, files_deleted, metrics doc rule).

## Notes

- Order (05.1-PATTERNS lesson "references first, files later"): every reader of a scene is edited before the scene is deleted; `.uid` companions are deleted with their scripts; the second gym run after the deletions must be clean.
- The player code reaches the pads only through group names (`wall_run_surface`, `wall_jump_surface`), never through the block's class, so the wall states still compile after this plan and simply find no surface; plan 19 removes them. test_wall_states and test_wall_surface spawn the pads, so they go here with them.
- test_kit_blocks keeps the wall surface block's contract until plan 23 deletes the block; its contract scene moves from the plain wall pad to the block's base scene here.
- test_energy_costs: every wall scenario spawns a pad (entry, tick, wall jump), so all of them go here; any config-only wall check left in it goes with the config fields in plan 20.
- `tests/fixtures.gd` `bone_to_wall_distance` loses its only user (test_wall_surface) here; it is deleted in plan 19.
- Group B / Phase 7 may bring a wall move back later; this is a user cut (D-81), not a deferral.
