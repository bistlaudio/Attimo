## Accomplishments

- Eight stage-table F2 fields in `MovementConfig` (Loader group) and `movement_default.tres`; `loader_stage_time` has a strictly positive range minimum (0.1) because it divides.
- Five pure `LoaderMath` functions: `stage_speed`, `shot_time`, `exit_speed` (`minf(maxf(own, stage), cap)`, no addition), `shot_reach`, `shot_apex`. The shot simulation steps in the order of `PlayerController._integrate`.

- `EventBus.loader_stage_reached(position, stage)` declared and documented; nothing emits it yet (plan 03).
- Metrics snapshot regenerated with the eight new fields.
