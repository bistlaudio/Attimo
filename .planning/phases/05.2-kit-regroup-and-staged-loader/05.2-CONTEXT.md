## Phase Boundary

Playtest round 2 on the 05.1 build. The phase:

1. replaces the straight gravity-free Loader with a **staged Loader** (3 fixed stages, slight gravity, auto release, speed lines);
2. fixes the **energy rule**: energy only ever switches energy-layer objects; energy-layer movement objects really cost; energy-core stages really deduct; spending is always visible;
3. gives the energy core new **energy-only outputs** (energy drawbridge, light bridge, light path) and the charge gate an **energy glider**;
4. retunes and re-roles a handful of kit objects and **cuts** the ones the user rejected;
5. **regroups the gym into 11 rooms**.

Not in this phase: removing the Jump 1-2-3 chain (deferred, see Deferred Ideas), final looks (Group B), health/enemies (Phase 6), audio beyond existing placeholder cues.

## Implementation Decisions

### Loader: staged, faster, shorter (replaces 05.1 D-xx straight flight formula)

- **D-63 Stages, not a formula.** Exit speed = `max(own speed, stage speed)` (no addition, no k x own speed, no overflow-to-distance). Stage speeds 15 / 18 / 21 m/s, cap stays 22 m/s. Cost stays 1 / 2 / 3 steps (1 step paid per stage as it is reached, refusal as today).
- **D-64 Slight gravity in the shot.** The flight is still a straight line in the view direction, but under 0.3x gravity for a stage-dependent shot phase of 0.30 / 0.40 / 0.50 s, then normal gravity and the speed carries on. No gravity-free flight distance any more.
- **D-65 Target reach.** ~8 / 12 / 16 m horizontal reach for stages 1 / 2 / 3, from standing and from a run alike (stage 3 is about the dash hop stage 3, 15.6 m). The numbers in D-63/D-64 are F2 starting values; tune toward D-65 and record the result in MOVEMENT-METRICS.md. Today's 3-step run reach (24.3 m) is the "too strong" baseline.
- **D-66 Clearly separated stages.** Each stage takes 0.25 s real time (start value). Reaching a stage is a distinct beat: the ring segment pops, a click, a pitch step in the hum, a small camera kick and one arm core lights. Between stages nothing creeps.
- **D-67 Auto release at full charge.** The moment stage 3 is reached the Loader fires (no wait for the hold cap). Releasing earlier fires the stage reached so far.
- **D-68 Flight FX.** Speed lines while flying (screen-space shader, reference https://godotshaders.com/shader-tag/speed-lines/ shared by the team) plus a turquoise trail. Scales with the stage. This is an explicit exception to the Group A "no juice" rule because the user asked for it as readability.
- **D-69 Slingshot core = fixed stage 2.** The cage fires the stage-2 discharge regardless of entry speed (weaker than today), free, +1 step. Its catch hitbox must match the visible cage: today Rino flies through it.
- **D-70 Zone_Loader** gap and ledge are re-derived from the new stage 1 vs stage 3 reach.

### Energy rule

- **D-71 Energy only switches energy objects.** Energy cores and the charge gate only ever drive turquoise energy-layer receivers. They never switch steampunk or earth objects. The switched speed pad (`speed_pad_switched.tscn`) goes; piston steps stay a plain steampunk platform. Steampunk boosters still give +1 step (steampunk feeding energy is allowed, energy steering steampunk is not).
- **D-72 Energy-layer movement objects cost** (supersedes "Amplified = reads the arm, never spends"):
  - **Bounce pad with core:** always costs (start: 1 step per bounce), because it is the stronger version of the free steampunk bounce pad. Refusal when empty: it acts like the plain bounce pad (or refuses, planner's call, document it).
  - **Attraction field:** no own cost any more; it costs indirectly because you have to dash out (dash costs). Remove the fast-escape EnergyCost.
  - **Loader field:** unchanged rule (needs a Loader of stage >= 2).
  - **Gravity zone:** see D-77 (becomes an obstacle).
  - With the gravity zone and bounce pad no longer reading the arm, the `Amplification` component loses its users: delete it (guardrail 3). `EnergyCost` must keep two users or be folded back (guardrail 3); the planner decides and records it.
- **D-73 Energy core stages really deduct.** Paying in removes the steps from the clock, every threshold (3 / 6 / 9) permanently unlocks its output. The user reported that "in the energy room nothing deducts": verify the core pay-in actually spends and make it read (see D-74). If it already deducts, the bug is readability.
- **D-74 Juicy spend feedback and visible costs.** Every spend shows a visual indicator (the clock hand klack plus pips/sparks flying from the source to the clock, small arm-core flash). Every energy object shows its cost before use (a cost badge with clock pips above it). The core's three stages must be clearly visible (rings/segments light one per stage, a distinct beat per stage).
- **D-75 Core and charge gate stay separate.** Core = permanent unlock by paying in. Charge gate = temporary: passing with >= X steps pays X and drives its receiver for N seconds.

### Energy outputs (all turquoise, never steampunk/earth)

- **D-76 Core outputs:** stage 1 **energy drawbridge** (the drawbridge reskinned turquoise; the steampunk drawbridge is removed from the steampunk rooms); stage 2 **light bridge** (turquoise floating plates build up one by one and stay); stage 3 **light path** (light plates appear one after another and despawn one after another shortly after, a timing run; whoever dawdles falls). Charge gate output: **energy glider** (a turquoise platform that, once the gate fired, carries Rino along a path he cannot cross otherwise). Rejected: time anchor, gravity lift, piston steps as output.

### Kit changes

- **D-77 Gravity zone = strong downward gravity, an obstacle** (energy obstacles room). No own cost; escaping costs indirectly (dash). No amplification.
- **D-78 Boost ring** always floats in the air and is weaker: its push only reaches as far as an air dash (~4 m).
- **D-79 Speed pad** smaller and compact; its push direction and its active surface must read clearly (arrows, a lit surface exactly where it acts).
- **D-80 Geyser and steam stream become obstacles** (steampunk obstacles room); the steam stream gets stronger. Both keep their force-volume block; they no longer give the +1 step steampunk income (they are obstacles, not boosters; planner confirms). The updraft stays a booster (steampunk movement room) with +1 step.
- **D-81 Cuts:** wall run and wall jump states plus wall pad / wall jump pad (plain and steam), rail cart, leaf storm, resin pool, snap bloom, the momentum gauge (speedometer) in the HUD, `Zone_KitCourse` (test parkour), `Zone_MovingPlatforms`, the switched speed pad, the old Zone_Energy stations (boost line, wall run, shot leftovers). The wall surface block goes with the wall pads (no users left). Cut-file gate test as in 05.1-22.
- **D-82 Keep as is:** clock-hand platform, tilting platform, bounce pad, steam cannon, updraft, steam hammer, piston steps, paternoster, pendulum, piston wall, bounce pad with core (now paid), broken machine, capsule, attraction field, Loader field, crumbling platform, tipping trunk, sinking debris, collapse, falling debris.

### HUD

- **D-83 HUD clock top centre, no speedometer.** The momentum gauge (speedometer) is removed completely (also listed in D-81). The energy clock moves from top left to **top centre** of the screen (horizontally centred, same size). The Moto Perpetuo state and the refusal shake move with it. The free slot reserved for health (Phase 6) sits next to the centred clock. (User, 2026-11-27.)

### Resolved after research (2026-11-27, orchestrator, recommended options; D-65 reach wins over the D-63/D-64 start values as D-63 allows)

- **D-84 Loader start values** for D-65: stage speeds **16.5 / 20 / 22 m/s**, shot phases **0.40 / 0.50 / 0.70 s** at 0.3x gravity (research simulation: 8.1 / 11.8 / 15.5 m at 10 degrees). Measuring pitch stays 10 degrees. Confirm with `measure_movement.gd`, tune on F2.
- **D-85 Stage timing:** stage 1 on the press, stage 2 at 0.25 s, stage 3 at 0.50 s real time, then auto fire. `hold_ratio()` is redefined over the 0.50 s so camera pull-back and vignette reach full strength.
- **D-86 Cage:** exactly the stage-2 speed and shot phase; a Loader field still blocks it (cage counts as stage 2 = passes). Swept catch with the radius sized to the visible ring.
- **D-87 Spend feedback:** a new EventBus fact `energy_spent_at(position, amount, reason)`; pips fly from the spend position in the world to the HUD clock. A `loader_stage_reached(stage)` fact drives the stage beats. All FX live in `scripts/fx`, `scripts/ui` or `scripts/camera` (test_feedback_guard).
- **D-88 EnergyCost** stays and gets three users (core pay-in tick, bounce pad core, charge gate) via a one-shot `pay_now`; the attraction field loses it; `Amplification` is deleted.
- **D-89 Charge gate:** X = 3 steps, N = 8 s (start values); the glider route time must stay below N (test asserts it). Bounce pad core when empty: acts like the plain bounce pad and shows the refusal once.
- **D-90 Geyser and steam stream** stay pure force volumes (no DamagePush), no +1 step income.
- **D-91 Charge indicator** (R) moves right next to the centred clock. Strong gravity zone uses a separate gravity-up accumulator in the controller (the low-gravity clamp stays for other users).
