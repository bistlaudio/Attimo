<objective>
Make the bounce pad with core really cost: one step per bounce through EnergyCost.pay_now, a higher bounce than the free pad, and the plain bounce with one refusal when the clock is empty. Delete the read-only Amplification component together with its second user's read (the gravity zone), so no energy object reads the arm any more.

Purpose: ENRG-RULE (D-72 bounce pad with core, D-88, D-89 empty-pad rule) and the Amplification deletion of D-72 / guardrail 3.
Output: paid bounce core, Amplification and its test gone, coupling and cut gate updated.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

Plan set notes: read "Plan set notes" in `05.2-01-PLAN.md` (environment, D-88, cut gate, sign texts, metrics doc rule).

## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **D-89 empty bounce core:** behaves exactly like the plain bounce pad (restitution 1.0, its speed floor) and refuses once per landing chain, so a drained player is never trapped on a pit pad.
- **Gravity zone in between:** removing Amplification takes the gravity zone's only arm read; for one plan it is a FREE low-gravity energy zone (its preset unchanged), plan 10 turns it into the strong-down obstacle (D-77).
- `KitBlock.EnergyRelation.AMPLIFIED` stays in the enum (serialized ints, `keys().size() == 4` and `keys()[3] == "GIVES"` are asserted); no object uses it after this plan.
- The deleted component's class name may only survive in test files that assert its absence (CUT_FILES, coupling test) and, until plan 13 rewrites it, in one header comment of tools/kit_energy_builder.gd; no script or scene names it.
- The gym signs of the bounce pad with core and the gravity zone are rewritten in plan 13 (plan set notes, sign texts); the preset value change of bounce_pad_core.tres reaches the metrics doc with its next regeneration (metrics doc rule).
