## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

Plan set notes: read "Plan set notes" in `05.2-01-PLAN.md` (environment, gym rule, metrics doc rule).

## Decisions recorded in this plan (Claude's discretion, CONTEXT)

- **Glider:** a PATH ping-pong deck, SWITCHED. New meaning of SWITCHED for a ping-pong PATH (no existing user): on = runs the route, off = travels back to its first point and rests, so the glider always waits at the gate. Start path_speed 5.0 m/s; the gap is the best stage-3 Loader reach over pitch 0..80 degrees (LoaderMath.shot_reach, about 26 m) plus 1 m, so it cannot be crossed any other way; the builder raises path_speed if the one-way route time would not stay at least 1 s under GATE_HOLD.
- `glider_course` replaces `bridge_course` for the gate station; `bridge_course`, `pad_course` and the steampunk signal objects are removed in plan 17 at the latest.
