<objective>
Switch the Loader from the straight, gravity-free, formula-driven flight to the staged Loader: three fixed stages reached in 0.25 s steps of real time, each paid with one step and announced by the fact of plan 01, an exit speed of max(own speed, stage speed) capped at 22 m/s, a short 0.3x-gravity shot phase per stage, and an automatic release the moment stage 3 is paid. Every reader of the flight (Loader field, attraction field, cage, Loader room) moves to the new flight status in the same plan, and Zone_Loader is re-derived and regenerated.

Purpose: LOAD-STAGE and LOAD-ROOM, plus the speed half of LOAD-CAGE. The core value of the project (smooth, fast movement) and the highest risk of the phase.
Output: rewritten LoaderState and flight in PlayerController, re-pointed readers, rewritten Loader tests, re-derived Zone_Loader.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a faster, shorter Loader with three clearly separated stages, energy that only ever drives turquoise objects and costs visibly, and a gym regrouped into clean rooms, **so that** the energy decisions read at a glance and the kit feels tight after the 2026-11-27 playtest.

Plan set notes: read "Plan set notes" in `05.2-01-PLAN.md` (environment, D-84 numbers, D-86 reading, measure tool between plans 03 and 04).
