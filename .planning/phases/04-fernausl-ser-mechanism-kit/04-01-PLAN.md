## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** aim the Fernauslöser pulse at glowing mechanisms, **so that** I can solve puzzles from a distance.

(ROADMAP still says "beam"; the developer chose a pulse, D-01. Plan 04-05 fixes the ROADMAP wording.)

This plan is the tracer bullet: aim, tap, a small pulse hits a lever, the lever opens the Zahnrad-Tor that is the door out of the movement gym. It also lays the contracts every later plan builds on (module framework, Interactable, SignalSource, Mechanism, all new EventBus facts).
