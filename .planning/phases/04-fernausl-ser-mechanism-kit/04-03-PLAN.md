**As a** player, **I want to** aim the Fernauslöser pulse at glowing mechanisms, **so that** I can solve puzzles from a distance.

This plan completes the trigger set and the logic nodes. After it, in room 2 the player can heave the valve with a big pulse, light an energy core for a timed gate, wind a crank by holding E, and open gates that need two levers (AND), either lever (OR) or a lever plus a timer.

Purpose: MECH-01 (trigger half), MECH-02, and the near-hold half of HAND-02 (D-10).
Output: three trigger scripts and scenes, three logic scripts and scenes, showcase stations, two test files.
