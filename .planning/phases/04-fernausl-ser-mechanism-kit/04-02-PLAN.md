## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** aim the Fernauslöser pulse at glowing mechanisms, **so that** I can solve puzzles from a distance.

This plan makes aiming readable and sticky: interactables glow, the goggle HUD shows target, charge and module, the assist snaps the pulse onto the lever, the hand shows its energy, and E works as near contact on the lever from plan 04-01.
