## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** aim the Fernauslöser pulse at glowing mechanisms, **so that** I can solve puzzles from a distance.

This plan makes the mechanisms move the player. Afterwards the player can ride a gear, a clock hand, a tilting plate, a lift and a conveyor without sliding or being launched. Steam nozzles throw the player up and a pendulum knocks them away. Pulse-triggered levers in room 2 start all of it.

<objective>
The plan does four things, in this order:
1. Settle the rotating-platform carry with a spike that acts as a go/no-go gate.
2. Add the narrow PlayerController entry points (launch_along, push_toward, knock_back) together with the lift and the steam nozzle.
3. Add the Förderband and the Pendel in their own task.
4. Place the showcase stations in room 2.

Purpose: MECH-03 and the moving half of MECH-01. D-20 and D-22 touch the core value.
Output: five test files, the PlayerController edits, five mechanism scripts and scenes, and the showcase stations.
</objective>
