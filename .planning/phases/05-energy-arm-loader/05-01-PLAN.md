## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have my arm store energy from speed and stop, aim and discharge my momentum with the right-click Loader, **so that** speed turns into energy and energy into more speed.

After this plan the player holds right click and the world slows to a quarter, Rino skids to a stop on the ground or hangs in the air, and on release Rino is thrown along the camera view. The old aim mode is gone; the always-on crosshair and the target assist stay.

<objective>
Plan 05-01 does the riskiest work first (LECTURE-PRINCIPLES checklist item 2): the Loader hold and release and the single time owner, then the complete aim-mode removal that frees the right mouse button.

1. Tracer: the Loader end to end through every layer it needs (Input Map, FSM state, TimeFx time owner, the minimal ArmEnergy bonus spend, real-time CameraRig, EventBus facts, docs whitelist, smoke tests).
2. Aim removal part A: the `aiming` flag and everything that reads it (controller, states, ProceduralPose, MannequinAnimator, HandController gate, Targeting gate and sticky slowdown, CameraRig sensitivity multiplier and wheel zoom).
3. Aim removal part B: the aim_changed fact and its listeners (CameraRig aim blend, goggle HUD, Interactable glow), the F2 aim section, the gym sign, the docs and a static guard test.

Purpose: HAND-04 (Loader core) and ENRG-04 (aim removed). Presentation of the Loader (rings, preview, vignette, room) is plan 05-02.
Output: TimeFx, LoaderMath, LoaderState, minimal ArmEnergy, aim-free player, camera, hand and HUD code, rewritten tests.
</objective>
