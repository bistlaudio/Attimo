## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have my arm store energy from speed and stop, aim and discharge my momentum with the right-click Loader, **so that** speed turns into energy and energy into more speed.

After this plan the movement gym shows the new core: from the Loader room the player runs into an energy area with a long charge track, capsules and a broken machine, a charge spot, one station per injected move with an empty and a charged try, and shot stations; every old shot puzzle has a capsule next to it. The metrics doc holds the energy rates, and the design docs match what was built.
