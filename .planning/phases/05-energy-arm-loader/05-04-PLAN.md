## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have my arm store energy from speed and stop, aim and discharge my momentum with the right-click Loader, **so that** speed turns into energy and energy into more speed.

After this plan left click fires from the hip with two modules on the wheel and keys 1/2: the Extractor drains glowing capsules and broken machines into the arm, and the shot is a flying projectile that costs 10 for a tap and up to 25 for a full Samus-style charge, bends into a locked target and opens heavy mechanisms only when fully charged.

<objective>
1. Tracer: Extractor end to end (energy source block, capsule scene, module folder + one registry line, hold callbacks, module keys) so a capsule fills the arm.
2. The shot as an energy-priced projectile with a hybrid charge and a PulseHit energy payload that keeps the Phase 4 size contract (costly decision D-17).
3. Readability and assist: stronger snap with bend, energy-gated glow, the notched clockwise shot ring with a full-charge tell, and the broken machine.

Purpose: ENRG-02 (capsules and machines), ENRG-04 (target aim assist stays, stronger), CONTEXT D-11, D-16..D-19, D-24, D-28.
Output: EnergySource, energy_capsule.tscn, broken_machine.tscn, Extractor module, ShotProjectile, rewritten shot module.
</objective>
