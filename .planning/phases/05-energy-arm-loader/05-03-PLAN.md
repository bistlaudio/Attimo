## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have my arm store energy from speed and stop, aim and discharge my momentum with the right-click Loader, **so that** speed turns into energy and energy into more speed.

After this plan the arm is a real battery: dashing, hopping and every fast technique fill it, the four cores on the forearm and the HUD show it, injected moves spend it, and from two cores Rino runs in Moto Perpetuo with glowing feet. Respawning always gives one core.

<objective>
1. Tracer: speed -> passive charge -> HUD chambers fill -> an air dash spends 10 or is refused below 10, end to end.
2. The rest of the governing rule: hop stage costs with silent fallback, wall run cost, Moto Perpetuo with hysteresis and its buffs, the charge button.
3. Presentation: the four arm cores, Moto glow points and the complete energy HUD with the momentum readout.

Purpose: ENRG-01, ENRG-02 (passive + charge button), ENRG-03, ENRG-05. Capsules and machines (the rest of ENRG-02) arrive with the Extractor in plan 05-04.
Output: full ArmEnergy, energy costs at the controller choke points, ArmEnergyPresentation, EnergyHud.
</objective>
