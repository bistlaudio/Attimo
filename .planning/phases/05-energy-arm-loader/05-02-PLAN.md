## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have my arm store energy from speed and stop, aim and discharge my momentum with the right-click Loader, **so that** speed turns into energy and energy into more speed.

After this plan the player walks through a new west door into the Loader room, holds right click and sees a turning double ring, a dashed arc of where the discharge goes, a turquoise vignette and a slightly pulled-back camera, then releases with a sharp hitstop. Gaps and a ledge in the room are sized so that only a discharge with a full core clears them. The Loader numbers are measured into the metrics doc.

<objective>
1. Tracer: the Loader room with the two readouts that make aiming a discharge possible (double ring, dashed preview), sized from the real metrics, with respawn to one core.
2. The "geladen" cue set (D-04): vignette and desaturation, camera pull-back, hitstop, rising hum.
3. Super jump: pitch raise during the hold (D-29), the ledge station, and the FEEL-10 Loader rows in MOVEMENT-METRICS.md.

Purpose: HAND-04 (charge ring, trajectory preview, free camera) and FEEL-10 (Loader metrics table). This is the focused Loader prototype the ROADMAP notes ask for, built before the energy economy.
Output: LoaderPreview, LoaderLook, PlaceholderTone, Zone_Loader, Loader metrics rows.

Group A exception (recorded, D-04): ROADMAP Group A forbids juice VFX and camera juice and places general hit-stop in Phase 11. The D-04 cue set (vignette, desaturation, slight camera pull-back, short release hitstop, rising hum) is a user-locked feel property of the Loader, not juice: each cue carries information the player needs to use the mechanic (the hold is active, how much is stored, the exact release moment), which the Group A rule allows ("feedback that carries information is part of the mechanic"), and ROADMAP Phase 5 criterion 4 plus GAME-DESIGN section 4 already require the slow motion, charge ring, desaturation and turquoise vignette. The cues stay greybox: procedural vignette, generator hum, one hitstop of about 0.05 s real time, a pull-back of at most 0.35 m, no particles and no camera shake. Phase 12 (Loader feedback) polishes them; the general hit-stop on damage and hits stays in Phase 11.
</objective>
