# Phase 04.1 Plan 01: Low-gravity tracer, request API foundation and conveyor fix Summary

**Low-gravity zone end to end through a new per-tick request_gravity_scale API, four more controller request APIs for the rest of the phase, and belts that finally carry the player (belt top lifted 0.05 m off the coplanar floor tile).**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 40 min
- **Tasks:** 3 (tracer, request APIs, conveyor fix and audit)
- **Files modified:** 15 (plus the regenerated gym)

## Accomplishments

- Tracer MECH-04 K: a lever in room 2 switches a LowGravityZone at (106, 0, 0); inside, a held full jump rises 5.72 m instead of 2.00 m and stays airborne 111 instead of 39 ticks; switching off restores the 2.00 m apex. The sign carries a second line "Erwartet: Sprünge hoch, weit und langsam".
- PlayerController now has request_gravity_scale, refill_air_dash, boost_along, lock_air_control and launch_along(direction, speed, keep_sideways), all with finite guards. A horizontal push in the air lifts air_speed_cap, so wind no longer gets dragged back.
- D-05: the showcase belts carry an idle rider at belt speed (3.00 m in 1 s at 3 m/s) and a jump off keeps 3.00 m/s on the first airborne tick.
- D-06: test_mechanism_influence proves lift (4.00 m), nozzle JUMP (apex 2.88 m), UPDRAFT (4.58 m), WALL (1.93 m) and pendulum (10 m/s) move the player, and a source guard over all 17 scripts under scripts/mechanisms finds no write to the player's motion fields and no move_and_slide.
