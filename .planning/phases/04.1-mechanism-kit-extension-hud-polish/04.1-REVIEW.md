# Phase 04.1: Code Review Report

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-23T12:00:00Z
**Depth:** standard
**Files Reviewed:** 49
**Status:** issues_found

## Summary

I reviewed the new player states (WallRun, WallJump, Capture), the PlayerController request API additions, all 14 new mechanism scripts and their scenes, the MechanismLook/GlowElement look pass, the HUD prompt and the builder changes. I checked the smoke tests only for whether they can catch the defects found here.

Checked and clean:
- **D-06:** No mechanism script writes the player's velocity, gravity_scale, position or FSM state. All movement goes through `launch_along`, `boost_along`, `knock_back`, `push_toward`, `request_gravity_scale`, `refill_air_dash` and `capture_along`. One-shots are deferred and per-tick requests are consumed by the controller.
- **Ready hooks:** Every new Mechanism or SignalSource subclass calls `super._ready()`, so `source.active_changed` is connected exactly once. The `NOTIFICATION_READY` look hook runs once per node, after the subclass `_ready` (engine order: C++ `_ready`, then the script `_notification`), so meshes built in `_ready` are already present when it runs.
- **Normalize and division safety:** No NaN or divide-by-zero path was found. `normalized()` on a zero vector returns zero in Godot, the request APIs reject zero or non-finite input, and Capture divides only by the physics delta.
- **Builder:** The builder does not add the gym root to a SceneTree, so `GlowElement._ready` does not run at build time and the scale is not doubled.

The main defect is WallRun's entry rule. It keeps any upward speed, so wall-running out of a held jump turns a 4 m pad into a launcher. That breaks the core-value movement feel. The other findings are an energy-gaining bounce pad, missing FSM cleanup on teleport, a WallRun that never re-checks that it is still on a pad, and GlowElement normals that are inverted on every face. I verified the inverted normals headlessly on Godot 4.7.2.

## Critical Issues

### CR-01: WallRun keeps the full jump rise, so a wall-run pad works as a super-jump

**File:** `attimo/scripts/player/states/wall_run.gd:23` (with `attimo/scripts/player/states/air_state.gd:26-27`)
**Issue:** `enter()` does `actor.vertical_speed = maxf(actor.vertical_speed, config.wall_run_entry_rise)`. It only raises the vertical speed and never caps it. `AirState.physics_update` checks `can_wall_run()` from the first airborne tick of Jump and LongJump as well as Fall. With `run_speed` 8.5 m/s, above `wall_run_min_speed` 7, a player who runs along a pad and presses jump enters WallRun on the next tick at about `jump_speed(2.0)` = 11.7 m/s, or 15.6 m/s from a stage-3 long jump.

Gravity during the run is `rise_gravity * wall_run_gravity_scale` = 34 * 0.1 = 3.4 m/s². WallRun applies no jump cut. The player therefore climbs about 4 m in 0.35 s, rises past the top of the 4 m pad, loses contact after `wall_contact_grace` and drops into Fall while still rising at about 10 m/s. Instead of running along the pad, the player is launched about 10 m or more into the air. The test (`test_wall_states.gd:76-80`) enters WallRun from a launch with little vertical speed, so it cannot catch this (see WR-07).
**Fix:**
```gdscript
# wall_run.gd enter(): a wall-run starts with a defined rise, never with the jump's take-off speed.
actor.vertical_speed = clampf(actor.vertical_speed, 0.0, config.wall_run_entry_rise)
# or simply: actor.vertical_speed = config.wall_run_entry_rise
```
Also add a test that runs along a pad, presses jump, and asserts that `vertical_speed <= wall_run_entry_rise` on the first WallRun tick and that the player stays below the pad top for `wall_run_time`.

## Warnings

### WR-01: Bounce pad gains energy on every bounce; an idle player pumps up to max_bounce

**File:** `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/bounce_pad.gd:54`
**Issue:** `bounce = clampf(impact_speed * restitution, min_bounce, max_bounce)` with `restitution = 1.0`. A launch at speed v rises under `rise_gravity` (34) and falls back under `fall_gravity()` (34 * 1.3 = 44.2). The impact speed is therefore v * sqrt(44.2 / 34) ≈ 1.14 v, and that becomes the next launch.

A player who does nothing bounces 11 → 12.5 → 14.3 → … m/s and reaches `max_bounce` = 30 m/s, about 13 m high, after roughly eight bounces. From then on the bounce never ends. This contradicts the station promise ("Je tiefer du fällst, desto höher springst du"): the height should depend on the drop, not grow by itself.
**Fix:** Compute the bounce from the drop height under the rise gravity, so that restitution 1.0 returns exactly to the drop height:
```gdscript
var config: MovementConfig = (player as PlayerController).config
var drop_height: float = impact_speed * impact_speed / (2.0 * config.fall_gravity())
var bounce: float = clampf(config.jump_speed(drop_height * restitution), min_bounce, max_bounce)
```
Alternatively, keep `restitution <= 1.0 / sqrt(fall_multiplier)`.

### WR-02: `teleport()` leaves the FSM state alone; a respawn during Capture or WallRun keeps running that state

**File:** `attimo/scripts/player/player_controller.gd:204-210`
**Issue:** `teleport` zeroes the motion but not `state_machine.current`. FallReset calls it deferred.
- **Capture:** If the respawn happens during Capture, `Capture.physics_update` keeps steering toward `capture_points`. The respawned player is flown back across the level toward the old path for up to `MAX_CAPTURE_TIME` (4 s). The stuck guard does not fire, because the distance keeps shrinking.
- **WallRun:** `move_velocity` is rewritten to `_tangent * _speed` with the reduced gravity_scale until `wall_contact_grace` runs out, so the player slides sideways right after the respawn.
- **Dash:** Dash keeps its burst for the same reason.
**Fix:**
```gdscript
func teleport(new_position: Vector3) -> void:
	global_position = new_position
	move_velocity = Vector3.ZERO
	vertical_speed = 0.0
	velocity = Vector3.ZERO
	_was_on_floor = false
	_enter_fall()  # exits Capture/WallRun/Dash through their exit(), which restores gravity_scale
	move_velocity = Vector3.ZERO  # exit() hooks may touch it
	vertical_speed = 0.0
	reset_physics_interpolation()
```

### WR-03: WallRun never re-checks the pad, so it keeps running on plain walls and on pads that were switched off

**File:** `attimo/scripts/player/states/wall_run.gd:52` (with `player_controller.gd:637-654`)
**Issue:** The only contact test during the run is `actor.wall_contact_age > config.wall_contact_grace`. `_track_wall_contact` resets that age for any near-vertical collider, not only pads. If the pad ends against a plain wall, or the player brushes a plain wall, the run continues for the full `wall_run_time`. The design says only pads count.

The same applies when the pad's signal goes off mid-run: `WallPad._apply(false)` removes the group, but WallRun never asks again. `_tangent` and `_normal` are also frozen at enter, so a run that slides onto a different wall pushes along a stale normal.
**Fix:** Check the surface every tick:
```gdscript
if _timer >= config.wall_run_time or not actor.wall_surface(&"wall_run_surface"):
	return &"Fall"
```
`wall_surface` already folds in `wall_contact_grace`.

### WR-04: GlowElement builds every face with an inverted normal (verified)

**File:** `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/glow_element.gd:166`
**Issue:** `_faceted` computes `face = (b - a).cross(c - a)`. Godot primitives use clockwise front-face winding, so this cross product points into the mesh. I verified this headlessly on Godot 4.7.2 by comparing the computed face normal with each primitive's own `ARRAY_NORMAL`:
- BoxMesh: 0 agree / 12 disagree
- CylinderMesh: 0 / 768
- TorusMesh: 0 / 4096
- PrismMesh: 0 / 8

Every core and every brass bezel is therefore lit from the wrong side: dark toward the sun and bright on the shadow side. The emissive core hides some of it, but the bezels (`look_brass.tres`, lit) are visibly wrong. Culling is unaffected because it follows the winding, which is why this was not noticed.
**Fix:**
```gdscript
var face: Vector3 = (c - a).cross(b - a)
```

### WR-05: `reset()` of the new mechanisms leaves them off while their source is still on (latent)

**File:** `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/mechanism.gd:43-44`, `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/wall_pad.gd:28-30` (same pattern in force_field, wind_tunnel, low_gravity_zone, slingshot_core, pneumatic_tube, geyser, crumbling_platform, piston_wall, boost_ring, bounce_pad, paternoster)
**Issue:**
- `Mechanism.reset()` sets `signal_on = false` without consulting `source`.
- Every showcase station is driven by an always-on `Driver<tag>` (`start_active = true`). During a FallReset sweep that driver's `SignalSource.reset()` calls `set_active(true)`, which emits nothing because the value is unchanged.
- The mechanism therefore stays dead: WallPad leaves its surface group, Geyser holds the charge, ForceField and WindTunnel hide and stop.

It is not reachable today, because `FallResetRoom2` has no `reset_root` and the puzzle rooms use none of the new scenes. It breaks as soon as a station is put under a sweeping reset, which is the documented pattern for puzzle rooms.
**Fix:** Re-sync from the source after the base reset:
```gdscript
func reset() -> void:
	signal_on = false
	if source != null:
		set_signal(source.is_active)
```
Then make sure every subclass applies its look from `signal_on` after `super.reset()`. For example, WallPad should call `_apply(signal_on)` instead of `_apply(false)`.

### WR-06: Wall-run lean axis sticks into a following ground dash

**File:** `attimo/scripts/player/procedural_pose.gd:153-161`
**Issue:** `_wall_axis` is cleared only when `lean_angle == 0.0`. If a wall-run ends on the floor and a ground dash starts before the lean has eased to exactly 0, `_lean_target_deg` switches to `dash_lean_deg`. Because `move_toward` heads to that non-zero target, `lean_angle` never passes 0. The `_wall_axis` branch keeps returning early, so the whole dash leans sideways around the wall axis instead of forward. WallRun → Run → Dash is a normal combo.
**Fix:** Drop the wall axis as soon as another state wants its own lean:
```gdscript
if _machine.current.name == &"WallRun":
	_wall_axis = player.wall_normal.cross(Vector3.UP)
elif lean_angle == 0.0 or _machine.current.name == &"Dash":
	_wall_axis = Vector3.ZERO
```

### WR-07: Tests cannot catch the defects above (D-06 guard gaps, WallRun entry)

**File:** `attimo/tests/smoke/test_mechanism_influence.gd:17-19`, `attimo/tests/smoke/test_wall_states.gd:76-80`
**Issue:**
- **D-06 guard:** `WRITE_PATTERN` requires `=` right after the field name, so component writes such as `player.move_velocity.y = 5.0` or `player.move_velocity.x += 1.0` pass the guard. The field list also omits `coyote_timer`, `jump_buffer_timer`, `air_control_lock_timer`, `capture_*` and `wall_*`, as well as `global_position` writes and `state_machine.transition_to` calls from mechanisms. Those also break D-06.
- **WallRun test:** It checks only the *change* in vertical speed over 0.5 s, and only for an entry with little rise. It passes even while CR-01 exists.
**Fix:**
- Extend the pattern to `\.\s*(fields)\b(\.[xyz])?\s*[-+*/]?=(?!=)`, add the missing fields, and add `global_position\s*=` and `transition_to\(` checks scoped to `player.` receivers.
- Add a WallRun-from-Jump assertion on the absolute `vertical_speed` (see CR-01).

## Info

### IN-01: One-shot requests end Capture without `capture_released`; WallRun.exit alters a fresh launch

**File:** `attimo/scripts/player/player_controller.gd:316,336,378,448-450`
**Issue:**
- **Capture:** `launch_along`, `boost_along` and `knock_back` call `_enter_fall()` from any state. If one lands during Capture (a bounce pad landing on the path, a piston or a ring near a slingshot), the capture ends with no `capture_released` event, no `EXIT_CONTROL_LOCK` and no refill. SlingshotCore and PneumaticTube recover through `START_GRACE`, so only listeners are affected.
- **WallRun:** `WallRun.exit()` runs after the launch has written `move_velocity` and subtracts the into-wall part of the new velocity.
**Fix:** Either ignore one-shots while `state_machine.current.name == &"Capture"`, or emit `capture_released` from `Capture.exit()`. In WallRun, drop the stick component inside `physics_update` instead of `exit()`.

### IN-02: MechanismLook keyword "spring" is dead and substring matching misfires

**File:** `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/mechanism_look.gd:13-14`
**Issue:**
- `"spring"` contains the brass keyword `"ring"`, which is checked first, so the copper entry `"spring"` can never match.
- Plain `contains` also matches `"arm"` in Alarm/Warm/Charm, `"cap"` in Capsule/Escape, `"rim"` in Primary/Trim and `"ring"` in String/Steering.
**Fix:** Match whole camel-case or underscore tokens, or check the longer keywords first.

### IN-03: Animator does not treat WallRun or Capture as airborne

**File:** `attimo/scripts/player/mannequin_animator.gd:75`
**Issue:** `AIRBORNE_STATES` lacks `WallRun`, so WallRun → Idle/Run on touchdown skips the Land overlay. Capture always leaves through Fall, so it is unaffected today.
**Fix:** Add `&"WallRun"`.

### IN-04: BouncePad loses its EventBus listener after a re-parent

**File:** `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/bounce_pad.gd:34,38-40`
**Issue:** The pad connects in `_ready`, which runs once, and disconnects in `_exit_tree`, which runs every time the node leaves the tree. After a remove and re-add the pad never bounces again.
**Fix:** Connect in `_enter_tree`, guarded by `is_connected`.

### IN-05: WallJump2 shows its back to the corridor

**File:** `attimo/tools/mechanism_rooms_builder.gd:297-298`
**Issue:** The comment says the two pads "face each other", but both use the identity basis. The inlay and Glow of `wall_pad.tscn` sit on local +Z (z 0.27 / 0.3), so the second pad at z +2 shows its plain back to the gap. This is visual only; collision is two-sided.
**Fix:** Pass `builder.call(&"_yaw", 180.0)` for `WallJump2`.

### IN-06: Push requests ratchet `air_speed_cap` for the rest of the airtime

**File:** `attimo/scripts/player/player_controller.gd:473-474`
**Issue:** Any airborne push raises the cap to the current horizontal speed, and the cap never comes back down until the next landing. One tick in a 14 m/s wind tunnel or a 12 m/s repulsor lets the player air-steer at that speed in any direction after leaving the volume.
**Fix:** Decay the cap toward `max(run_speed, horizontal_speed())` once no push was applied this tick, or raise it only along the push direction.
