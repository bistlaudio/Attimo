## Locked decisions

### HUD and readability
- **D-01 Crosshair always visible.** Outside aim mode show a small centre dot / thin ring only. The full goggle HUD (rim arcs, notches, charge arc, module frame) stays aim-only. Firing rules stay as they are (D-02 of phase 4: nothing fires without aim). File today: `attimo/scripts/ui/crosshair.gd` (hidden unless `EventBus.aim_changed`).
- **D-02 "E" prompt.** When a near-contact interactable (levers, crank, pressure plates etc. with `accepts_near`) is within `hand_near_range`, a small "E" key prompt floats over its `target_point()`. Only the nearest one shows. It hides during near contact and when out of range.
- **D-03 Expectation line on signs.** Every showcase station sign gets a second line `Erwartet: <what should happen>` (German, short). Applies to the existing phase 4 stations and to all new stations.
- **D-04 Showcase sub-zones.** Split the showcase room into visible zones: Trigger, Logik, Dampf, Plattformen, Gefahren, plus zone(s) for the new mechanisms (e.g. Felder & Schleuder, Wand, Sprung & Tempo, Umwelt). Each zone: own floor tint, low divider beams between zones, a large zone header sign. **The showcase must feel spacious, not cramped (user, 2026-11-23):** make the room clearly bigger, wider (z) and longer (x), with generous spacing between stations (well above the 6 m aim-assist minimum, target about 10-14 m) and open floor around each station so the player can run up, test and fall/land safely. Room 2 grows east and wider (the puzzle rooms then shift; keep the builder metadata and `test_mechanism_rooms` green). All of it is generated by the builders (`attimo/tools/mechanism_rooms_builder.gd`), never hand-edited.

### Fixes
- **D-05 Conveyor carry bug.** Belts do not transport the player today. `Conveyor` only sets `StaticBody3D.constant_linear_velocity`; the PlayerController does not pick it up (it uses `PLATFORM_ON_LEAVE_DO_NOTHING` and its own carry logic for rotating platforms). Fix it so riding the belt moves the player at belt speed, and jumping off keeps the momentum (MOVE-08). Add/extend a smoke test that fails today.
- **D-06 Audit player influence.** Every mechanism that should move the player (nozzles, pendulum, conveyor, platforms, all new ones) must do so through PlayerController request APIs (`push_toward`, `launch_along`, `knock_back`, or new ones in the same style). Mechanisms never write the player's velocity directly. Verify each existing one actually affects the player in a smoke test.

### Visual
- **D-07 Lens restyle.** The turquoise glow sphere ("lens", `SphereMesh_lens` with `glow_*` materials) on levers, valve, crank, energy core, gear gate, lift, nozzle etc. looks like a plain round blob. Replace it with a steampunk element built from primitives: a brass bezel / gear-toothed ring framing a glowing turquoise core (faceted, low-poly). The glow stage materials (idle / aim / target) still switch on the core. Keep it one shared scene/resource so every interactable reuses it.
- **D-07b Light visual pass on ALL showcase assets (user, 2026-11-23).** Not only the lens: every mechanism should look a bit better and more varied, with its glow element shaped to fit its mechanic instead of the same circle everywhere (e.g. a lever gets a slotted lamp, the valve a spoked handwheel hub, the energy core a crystal or cage, the nozzle a vented ring, the pressure plate a glowing grate, the fields a cage or orb with rings). **Time budget: small.** Primitives only (CSG / Box / Cylinder / Prism / Torus), a few shared brass, copper and iron materials plus the turquoise energy material, no new imported assets, no custom shaders. At most one plan task for this; final art comes in Phase 10.

### New mechanisms (each gets a showcase station, sign with "Erwartet:", and a smoke test)
- **A Repulsor field:** energy sphere that pushes the player radially away while active.
- **B Attractor field:** energy sphere that pulls the player radially toward its centre while active.
- **C Slingshot core (point-symmetric):** the player who enters is pulled to the centre and ejected on the opposite side; the exit direction is the entry direction mirrored through the centre point (enter from upper left, leave to lower right). Speed is kept or boosted. The player must play with entry angles.
- **D Wall-run pad:** a glowing wall panel; touching it with enough horizontal speed starts a wall-run along the panel (no or reduced gravity, timed). **MOVE-12 is now IN SCOPE.** Animation workaround: run clip with a tilt via `ProceduralPose` lean, no new clip.
- **E Wall-jump pad:** a wall panel; in the air against it, jump kicks the player off away from the wall (and up).
- **F Bounce pad / trampoline:** bounce height scales with the incoming fall speed (with a minimum bounce).
- **G Boost ring:** flying through refills the dash and gives a speed boost along the ring axis.
- **H Pressure plate:** new trigger (SignalSource), on while the player stands on it.
- **I Crumbling platform:** shakes, falls shortly after being stepped on, respawns after a delay.
- **J Wind tunnel:** horizontal constant wind volume that lengthens or brakes jumps.
- **K Low-gravity zone:** gravity scaled down inside the volume, jumps become far and slow.
- **Steampunk extras (planner invents 3-5)**, for example: steam piston punch wall (piston slams out and shoves the player), pneumatic tube (enter, get transported to the exit), rotating cog stairs, pressure-valve geyser (charges and erupts periodically), clockwork elevator / paternoster, magnetic rail. Pick ones that fit the steampunk world and the movement core value.
