## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a richer set of steampunk mechanisms that move me (fields, slingshot, wall-run and wall-jump pads, bounce pads, boost rings, wind, low gravity and more) and a clearer hand HUD, **so that** the showcase reads at a glance and the world itself becomes part of my movement.

After this plan the player can walk into the showcase, pulse a lever and float through a low-gravity zone whose sign says what to expect. The belts finally carry the player, and every mechanism that moves the player is proven to do so through the controller's request API.

<objective>
The plan does three things, in this order:
1. Tracer: one new mechanism (K, low-gravity zone) end to end. It goes through a new controller request API, a mechanism script and scene, a showcase station with lever and a two-line sign ("Erwartet: ...", D-03), a regenerated gym and a smoke test.
2. The remaining request APIs that plans 04.1-02 and 04.1-03 build on (refill_air_dash, boost_along, lock_air_control, launch_along keep_sideways, air cap raise after pushes), test-first.
3. The conveyor carry fix (D-05) with a guard test that fails before the fix, plus the D-06 influence audit test.

Purpose: MECH-04 K plus the request-API foundation for the whole phase (D-06). It also fixes the D-05 conveyor bug.
Output: LowGravityZone, five controller APIs, two shared energy materials, the expectation-line helper, conveyor fix, three test files.
</objective>
