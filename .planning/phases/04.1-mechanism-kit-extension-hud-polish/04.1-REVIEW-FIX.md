### CR-01: WallRun keeps the full jump rise, so a wall-run pad works as a super-jump

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification
**Files modified:** `attimo/scripts/player/states/wall_run.gd`, `attimo/tests/smoke/test_wall_states.gd`
**Commit:** 64246b1
**Applied fix:** `WallRun.enter()` now sets `vertical_speed = wall_run_entry_rise` instead of `maxf(vertical_speed, wall_run_entry_rise)`. Entries at or below the rise (falling entries included) behave exactly as before; only the faster jump take-off is capped. No movement number changed, so `docs/MOVEMENT-METRICS.md` (a static config table) needs no regeneration. New scenario `_scenario_run_entry_from_jump`: a take-off with flat 10 m/s and 11 m/s rise next to the pad must enter WallRun with `vertical_speed <= wall_run_entry_rise` and climb less than 1 m. Against the old code it failed (7.54 m/s on entry); with the fix it passes (1.44 m/s, 0.16 m climb).

### WR-01: Bounce pad gains energy on every bounce

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification
**Files modified:** `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/bounce_pad.gd`, `attimo/tests/smoke/test_pads_and_plate.gd`
**Commit:** 5de4642
**Applied fix:** The bounce now comes from the drop height: `drop = impact^2 / (2 * fall_gravity)`, `bounce = clamp(jump_speed(drop * restitution), min_bounce, max_bounce)`. The player lookup is typed (`as PlayerController`) and null-guarded. `restitution` now means a share of the drop height (doc comment updated; no scene or builder overrides it). New check: a 6 m drop returns about 6 m (measured 6.20 m), not more.

### WR-02: `teleport()` leaves the FSM state alone

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification
**Files modified:** `attimo/scripts/player/player_controller.gd`, `attimo/tests/smoke/test_wall_states.gd`
**Commit:** b8b6488
**Applied fix:** `teleport()` hands the FSM to Fall through `_enter_fall()` (guarded against a missing state machine), so Capture, WallRun and Dash end through their `exit()` and restore `gravity_scale`. The motion is zeroed after the hand-over and `reset_physics_interpolation()` stays last. Note: every teleport now goes through Fall, so a teleport onto the floor ends with a normal landing. New scenario `_scenario_teleport_ends_run` covers the WallRun case. The platforms, movement gym, mechanism rooms and slingshot/tube tests (all teleport users) pass.

### WR-03: WallRun never re-checks the pad

### WR-05: `reset()` of the new mechanisms leaves them off while their source is on

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification
**Files modified:** `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/mechanism.gd`, `attimo/scripts/mechanisms/wall_pad.gd`, `attimo/tests/smoke/test_wall_states.gd`
**Commit:** 5376592
**Applied fix:** This deviates from the reviewer's suggestion on purpose. `Mechanism.reset()` re-reads the source silently (`signal_on = source.is_active if source != null else false`) instead of calling `set_signal()`. Calling `set_signal()` would fire `_on_signal(true)` during a sweep. Because FallReset resets in tree order, a mechanism can read a stale lever value, and `SteamNozzle._on_signal(true)` launches any overlapping body. A source that resets later still corrects the value through `active_changed`. Every subclass already rebuilds its look from `signal_on`/`is_active()` after `super.reset()`, except WallPad, which now calls `_apply(signal_on)`. New check: `pad.reset()` with an active source keeps the pad on. The mechanism_rooms sweep invariant (signal_on == source.is_active) passes.
