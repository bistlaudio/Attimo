# Phase 04.1 Plan 02: Wall states, Schleuderkern and Rohrpost Summary

**Wall-run and wall-jump as pad-only FSM states with a roll lean, plus one shared Capture state that powers the point-symmetric Schleuderkern and the Rohrpost, with wall-run officially in scope.**

## Accomplishments

- MOVE-12: jumping at an active wall-run pad with at least 7 m/s of horizontal speed starts WallRun (11 m along the pad in 1.1 s at 0.1 gravity, a 6 m pad ends it after about 0.7 s, cooldown 0.35 s). A 4 m/s approach and a plain wall never start it. Jump during the run or in the air against a wall-jump pad kicks off (out 9 m/s, apex 2.6 m, air control locked 0.18 s).
- Presentation without a new clip: ProceduralPose tilts the Mannequin 18 degrees toward the wall during WallRun and rolls back within 0.3 s; WallRun shows `Locomotion`, WallJump `Jump`, Capture `Fall`.
- MECH-04 C: the Schleuderkern pulls the player to its centre (14 m/s), holds 0.12 s and throws along the point mirror of the entry offset (entry from upper left leaves to lower right, dot 1.000), exit speed entry times 1.15 clamped to 12..26 m/s, exit pitch never below -20 degrees (from straight above it measured -20.00).
- MECH-05 tube: walking into the mouth carries the player along P0..P3 at 16 m/s and fires them out at 14 m/s along the last leg (exit point reached within 0.09 m). A slab over the mouth releases the player after 38 ticks.
- Scope documents updated: MOVE-12 moved from v2 to v1 and CAM-06, HAND-07, MECH-04, MECH-05 added (v1 total 76), the v2 third module is HAND-08, and PROJECT.md, STACK.md and CLAUDE.md no longer say wall-run is out of scope.

**3. [Plan interpretation] can_wall_jump checks wall_jump_surface only**
- The action text allowed wall_run_surface too, but the plan's truths and the test list ("a WALL_RUN-only pad without speed does not enter WallJump") require the narrower rule. A jump during WallRun is handled by the state itself.

**4. [Plan interpretation] Capture hold at the end and stuck measure**
- The stuck guard measures progress as distance gained toward the current point (not a fixed 0.05 m over 0.5 s window), so a block releases 0.5 s after it happens. The hold phase closes the last gap and is exempt.
