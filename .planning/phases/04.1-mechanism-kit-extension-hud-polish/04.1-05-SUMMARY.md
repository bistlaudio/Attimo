## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Test filter matched the wrong `Divider_*` nodes**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** puzzle room 2 owns `Divider_<z>` wall nodes, so the divider checks reported five "off border" dividers at x 233.5.
- **Fix:** the divider checks only look under ZoneDecor.
- **Files modified:** attimo/tests/smoke/test_showcase_layout.gd
- **Commit:** cae522a

**2. [Plan interpretation] Bounce ledge is 4 m (unit steps), not 6 m**
- A 6 m ledge needs steps of 2 m, which equal the single-jump apex (2.00 m) and are not reliably climbable. The ledge is four stacks of 1, 2, 3 and 4 floor tiles west-to-east (top 4 m, 3.7 m drop onto the 0.3 m pad); the 04.1-03 test already proved bounce heights for a 6 m drop. Easy to raise later by adding steps.

**3. [Plan interpretation] Phase-4 stations keep their original lever offsets**
- Stations carried over from phase 4 (nozzles, rotating platforms, lifts, belts, pendulum) keep their old lever position relative to the station point (for example 5.5 m south of a rotating platform) instead of the uniform (-4, 0, 4): an 8 m rotating plate has its corner exactly at (-4, 4). New stations use (-4, 0, 4).
- Logic stations (UND, ODER) place their gate 4 m toward +z instead of +x because their levers stand at x ±2 and the 4 m gate would overlap lever B.

**4. [Plan interpretation] Rohrpost geometry**
- Fixed tube path (10 m, exit at 3 m height) did not fit the 24 x 24 m Felder zone without hitting a neighbour. The tube points along +x (yaw -90) from (77, 0, -18) with `exit_speed` 6 so the exit lands about 2 m before the zone edge; the station point stays on the slot (85, 0, -18).

**5. [Plan interpretation] Ray guard threshold**
- The gym holds 18 guarded moving bodies (12 in the showcase, 6 in the puzzle rooms), so the sanity threshold is 15 instead of the plan's implied 20.

**Total deviations:** 1 auto-fixed, 4 interpretation notes. **Impact:** none on scope.
