## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a richer set of steampunk mechanisms that move me (fields, slingshot, wall-run and wall-jump pads, bounce pads, boost rings, wind, low gravity and more) and a clearer hand HUD, **so that** the showcase reads at a glance and the world itself becomes part of my movement.

After this plan the player can run along glowing wall pads and kick off wall-jump pads. A Schleuderkern catches them and throws them out through the mirror point, and a Rohrpost carries them through a brass tube and fires them out of the other end. Wall-run is officially in scope.

<objective>
The plan does four things, in this order:
1. Wall-run slice: wall-contact bookkeeping, the WallRun state, the WallPad mechanism, a Wall config group and the animation map row.
2. Wall-jump plus presentation: the WallJump state, the roll lean in ProceduralPose and the animator mapping.
3. Capture slice: the capture_along request, the Capture state, the Schleuderkern (C) and the Rohrpost (MECH-05 extra).
4. Scope documents: MOVE-12 into v1, the new requirement IDs, HAND-07/HAND-08 renumbering, and the wall-run notes in PROJECT.md, STACK.md and CLAUDE.md.

Purpose: MOVE-12, MECH-04 C/D/E and the tube of MECH-05. These are new movement verbs that touch the core value, so the FSM stays the source of truth.
Output: three FSM states, three mechanisms with scenes, two test files and the doc updates. Showcase stations for these mechanisms are placed by plan 04.1-05.
</objective>
