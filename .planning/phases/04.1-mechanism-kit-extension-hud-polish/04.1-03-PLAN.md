## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a richer set of steampunk mechanisms that move me (fields, slingshot, wall-run and wall-jump pads, bounce pads, boost rings, wind, low gravity and more) and a clearer hand HUD, **so that** the showcase reads at a glance and the world itself becomes part of my movement.

After this plan the world pushes back. Fields push and pull, wind carries jumps, pads bounce, and rings boost and recharge the dash. A plate opens a gate while the player stands on it, and platforms crumble away. Three steampunk extras join them: the Kolbenwand, the Druckventil-Geysir and the Paternoster.

<objective>
The plan does three things, in this order:
1. Fields and wind: the repulsor and attractor (one ForceField script, two modes) and the wind tunnel, added to test_fields_and_zones.
2. Pads and plate: the bounce pad, boost ring, pressure plate and crumbling platform (test_pads_and_plate).
3. Steampunk extras (D-11): the piston punch wall, pressure-valve geyser and paternoster (test_extras). The pneumatic tube is in 04.1-02 because it shares the Capture state.

Purpose: MECH-04 A, B, F, G, H, I, J and MECH-05 (3 of 4 extras). Every mechanism is 40-100 lines, calls only request APIs and has a smoke test.
Output: nine mechanism scripts and scenes and three test files. Showcase stations are placed by 04.1-05.
</objective>
