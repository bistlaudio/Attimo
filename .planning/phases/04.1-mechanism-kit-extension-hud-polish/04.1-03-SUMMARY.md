# Phase 04.1 Plan 03: Fields, pads and steampunk extras Summary

**Nine request-API-only mechanisms: repulsor and attractor fields, wind tunnel, bounce pad, boost ring, pressure plate, crumbling platform, Kolbenwand, Druckventil-Geysir and Paternoster, each with a scene and a 60 Hz smoke test.**

## Accomplishments

- MECH-04 A and B: ForceField with Mode REPEL and ATTRACT. A repulsor pushes an airborne player more than 2 m radially away within 1 s. The attractor pulls to the centre with an approach speed limited by what the pull can brake and damps sideways speed, so after 2 s the player rests at the core radius (3D speed under 3 m/s).
- MECH-04 J: WindTunnel, REVERSE_ON_SIGNAL by default. A run-jump with tailwind lands at least 2 m farther and with the lever-flipped headwind at least 1 m shorter than without a tunnel.
- MECH-04 F: BouncePad on EventBus.landed with a footprint and height check, bounce = impact * restitution clamped to 11..30. A 6 m drop bounced 8.06 m high, a 2 m drop 2.81 m, a 0.5 m hop 1.78 m (the min_bounce apex).
- MECH-04 G: BoostRing with a 1.2 m thick cylinder trigger. 8 m/s leaves at 20 m/s, 26 m/s stays at 26, -X boosts along -X, the air dash is refilled, and two entries within the rearm time count once.
- MECH-04 H and I: PressurePlate is a SignalSource polled every tick (active within 2 ticks of standing, inactive within 3 ticks after leaving, opens a GearGate). CrumblingPlatform runs IDLE, SHAKE, FALL, GONE on tick accumulators and waits for an empty footprint before it respawns.
- MECH-05 (D-11): PistonWall telegraphs, slams and knocks exactly once per slam (12 m/s along the push direction). Geyser charges (gauge needle follows charge_ratio), erupts and launches each player in the column once per eruption (apex 8.47 m). Paternoster carries a rider 4.26 m up in 3 s with a largest feet-to-deck gap of 0.000 m.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Attractor orbited and overshot the centre**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the plan's linear speed fade toward the core ignored braking distance, and the radial pull never removed sideways velocity (gravity), so the player swung through the centre at 15 to 18 m/s.
- **Fix:** approach speed limited to sqrt(2 * pull_accel * 0.35 * distance) and an extra push toward zero on the sideways velocity component (read-only access to the player's velocity).
- **Files modified:** attimo/scripts/mechanisms/force_field.gd
- **Commit:** 498ef62

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] A headwind of 25 m/s^2 was cancelled by a held run key**
- **Fix:** default wind_accel raised to 60 (above air_accel 45) with a doc comment.
- **Files modified:** attimo/scripts/mechanisms/wind_tunnel.gd
- **Commit:** 498ef62

**3. [Plan interpretation] Repulsor test uses an airborne player**
- A grounded player is held by ground friction and cannot be pushed horizontally (the same as the steam wall test). The scenario places the field at y 3 and measures horizontal distance without a floor.

**4. [Plan interpretation] No "walk onto the pad from the side" bounce scenario**
- The pad top is raised 0.3 m (coplanar rule), so a grounded player cannot walk onto it. Replaced by "a landing next to the pad does not bounce" and "source off does not bounce".

**5. [Plan interpretation] Geyser and piston scenarios place the player so the effect is checkable**
- The piston player stands 1.5 m ahead of the retracted front face; the paternoster test floats on its own deck (no floor) because the lowest deck needs a raised base.

**Total deviations:** 2 auto-fixed, 3 interpretation notes. **Impact:** none on scope.
