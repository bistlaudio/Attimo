## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a richer set of steampunk mechanisms that move me (fields, slingshot, wall-run and wall-jump pads, bounce pads, boost rings, wind, low gravity and more) and a clearer hand HUD, **so that** the showcase reads at a glance and the world itself becomes part of my movement.

After this plan the player walks into a big, open showcase hall. Coloured zones with large headers group the mechanisms, every station has room to run up, land and fall, and every sign says what should happen. Every mechanism of the phase can be tried there, and the puzzle rooms still follow behind it.

<objective>
The plan does three things, in this order:
1. Room and zones: the 120 m x 72 m shell, the slot grid, nine zones with tint, dividers and headers, the puzzle-room shift, the west plug and the room-2 FallReset.
2. All 33 stations on the grid with two-line signs (D-03), drivers for the self-triggered mechanisms, station metadata, the layout test with the coplanar ray guard, and the gym-test kind counts.
3. Phase gate: the load-cost check with the D-12 fallback rule, the full suite, and the end-of-phase human check.

Purpose: D-03, D-04 and D-12, plus the "every new mechanism gets a showcase station" part of MECH-04, MECH-05 and MOVE-12.
Output: rebuilt builder workers, a regenerated gym, a new layout test, and updated gym and puzzle-room tests.
</objective>
