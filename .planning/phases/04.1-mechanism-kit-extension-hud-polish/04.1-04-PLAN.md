## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have a richer set of steampunk mechanisms that move me (fields, slingshot, wall-run and wall-jump pads, bounce pads, boost rings, wind, low gravity and more) and a clearer hand HUD, **so that** the showcase reads at a glance and the world itself becomes part of my movement.

After this plan the player always sees where the screen centre is, and an "E" keycap tells them when a lever or crank can be grabbed. Every mechanism looks like a small steampunk machine instead of a grey box with a turquoise ball.

<objective>
The plan does two things:
1. HUD (CAM-06): an always-on reticle plus an aim-only goggle HUD (D-01), and the world-anchored "E" near-contact prompt (D-02).
2. Look pass (HAND-07, D-07, D-07b) in exactly one task, as D-07b requires: shared materials, one GlowElement scene with per-mechanic styles, the four sphere lenses replaced, and a fitting glow element on every mechanism and trigger scene.

Purpose: CAM-06 and HAND-07.
Output: crosshair, hand-controller and test changes; GlowElement, MechanismLook and three materials; edits to every mechanism scene; two new test files.
</objective>
