# Phase 04.1 Plan 04: HUD and look pass Summary

**Always-on reticle with aim-only goggle HUD, an E keycap over the nearest grabbable lever or crank, and a steampunk look pass: seven faceted GlowElement styles plus shared brass, copper and iron materials on every mechanism.**

## Accomplishments

- CAM-06: `Crosshair.hud_visible` replaces visibility. Outside aim only a dot and a thin ring (idle_ring_radius 5, idle_alpha 0.6) are drawn; the rim, notches, charge arc and module frame appear only while aiming. `HandController.prompt_target` / `prompt_point()` track the nearest accepting interactable in range (cleared during contact); the `NearPrompt` keycap is placed with `camera.unproject_position(point + UP * 0.35)` and hidden when out of range, behind the camera, or in contact. Both Reticle and NearPrompt ignore the mouse. Firing logic untouched.
- HAND-07: `GlowElement` (DIAMOND, HUB, COG, CAGE, RING, GRATE, CHEVRON) builds a faceted core and a brass `Bezel` child from primitive meshes with hard normals, cached per style. Lever, valve, crank and energy core lenses are now GlowElements named GlowLens (DIAMOND, HUB, COG, CAGE), glow stages still switch their material_override. All other 22 scenes carry a `Glow` element (`GlowLamp` in slingshot_core, whose `Glow` name was taken) with glow_energy. `MechanismLook` gives plain metal meshes shared iron, brass or copper by node-name keyword.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] MechanismLook hook on NOTIFICATION_READY**
- **Issue:** most Mechanism subclasses override `_ready` without `super._ready()`, so a call in the base `_ready` would not reach them.
- **Fix:** `_notification(NOTIFICATION_READY)` in Mechanism and SignalSource calls `MechanismLook.apply(self)`.
- **Commit:** 734563f

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] test_logic_nodes asserted logic nodes have no mesh**
- **Issue:** the plan adds a DIAMOND lamp to logic_and/or/timer, which contradicts the old assertion.
- **Fix:** the check now allows only a GlowElement mesh.
- **Commit:** 734563f

**3. [Plan interpretation] Extra and adjusted keywords and exclusions**
- Added keywords (belt, housing, pillar, slat, axle, dial, rod, spring) and skip glowing/translucent meshes so steam, telegraph and energy parts keep their look. Glow element placements, sizes and orientations per scene are chosen by hand ([ASSUMED], tune by eye in the 04.1-05 feel check).
