## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** run, sprint, jump, double jump and dash with a smooth camera, **so that** moving feels fluid and fast.

This plan adds the original ground combo of D-05/D-06: Jump 1 -> land -> Jump 2 (higher) -> Dash -> Jump = the maximum long jump, with smaller long jumps for shorter chains, and the D-07 reset rules.

<objective>
Track the ground-jump chain in PlayerController (stage, landing window, reset on timeout or standing still, chain_stage_changed fact), make Jump 2 higher, and add the LongJump state that a jump during or just after a ground dash produces, sized by the chain stage from the tunable rows. Prove the whole combo and its edges in test_long_jump.

Purpose: MOVE-07 ("a dash can flow into a jump") in the shape the user decided, and the chain values that D-21 wants in the metrics table.
Output: chain logic, Jump stage heights, LongJump state and node, dash and ground hand-offs, test_long_jump.
</objective>

Chain rules (D-05/D-07, Claude's discretion pinned by test_long_jump):
  - A ground jump (Jump state, including coyote and buffered landing jumps) inside an open window at stage 1 is Jump 2 (stage 2, chain_jump_height); otherwise it is Jump 1 (stage 1, jump_height), also at stage 2 (A8: new chain).
  - Landing with stage > 0 opens the window (chain_timer = chain_window).
  - While grounded the window counts down; at 0 the stage resets to 0. Grounded and horizontal speed below chain_reset_speed (outside a dash) also resets to 0.
  - A ground dash inside the window keeps the stage and refreshes the window; an air dash and the double jump never change the stage (A1).
  - Every stage change emits chain_stage_changed(stage).

## Flagged Assumptions (non-blocking; the developer may veto at review)

- **A1 (RESEARCH Open Question 1 / A7)** The "Dash" in D-05 is a ground dash inside the landing window. An air dash between jumps is a separate air option and does not carry a chain stage into a long jump. Cheap to change: one condition in Dash and the controller.
- **A8** The chain caps at stage 2. A ground jump at stage 2 inside the window starts a new chain (Jump 1), so the rhythm is Jump 1 -> Jump 2 -> Dash -> Jump.
- **A30** A ground dash that runs off a ledge can still be cancelled into the long jump while coyote time lasts; once coyote is over a jump press becomes the double jump.
- **A31** The long jump direction is the dash direction; take-off speed and height come only from the three tunable rows (Pitfall 12: no stacking of dash speed).
