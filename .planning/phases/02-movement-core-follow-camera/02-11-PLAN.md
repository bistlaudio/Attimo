## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** run, sprint, jump, double jump and dash with a smooth camera, **so that** moving feels fluid and fast.

This plan assembles everything into the movement gym (D-20), makes it the main scene, and carries the end-of-phase sign-off: movement and camera are judged in the gym without any juice (success criterion 5).

<objective>

Purpose: Success criteria 1-5 are stated "in a movement gym"; the gym is also the tuning ground for MOVEMENT-METRICS.md.
Output: tools/build_movement_gym.gd, scenes/test/movement_gym.tscn, main scene switch, test_movement_gym, adjusted test_scene_run.
</objective>
