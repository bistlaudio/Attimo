## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** run, sprint, jump, double jump and dash with a smooth camera, **so that** moving feels fluid and fast.

This plan supplies the two missing Prototype Bits shapes the movement gym needs for success criterion 4 (slopes and stairs): the 45 degree Slope and the Beam (stair risers and ledges), with correct collision wrappers and the hygiene guards updated. It needs no movement code, so it runs in wave 1 next to the tracer; 02-07 proves the player on these pieces and 02-14 builds the gym from them.
