## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** run, sprint, jump, double jump and dash with a smooth camera, **so that** moving feels fluid and fast.

This plan turns the tracer's plain jump into the forgiving, readable jump of the success criteria: variable height, heavier fall, coyote time, a jump buffer that keeps speed, and exactly one double jump per airtime.

<objective>
Add the jump timers and decision helpers to PlayerController, route every jump through them in GroundState and AirState, cut the jump on release, and add the DoubleJump state with the double_jumped fact. Prove every edge (tap vs hold, tick-rate independence, coyote, buffer, one double jump) in test_jump.

Purpose: MOVE-03/04/05 are the core of "jumps feel forgiving". The rule set below is the Claude's-discretion part of D-08/D-09, written down so tests can pin it.
Output: controller timers and helpers, updated GroundState/AirState/Jump, new DoubleJump state and node, test_jump.
</objective>

Added to AirState:
  func apply_jump_cut() -> void                     # variable height for rising jump states

Jump decision rules (D-08/D-09, Claude's discretion, pinned by test_jump):
  1. Grounded, or airborne with coyote time left -> ground jump (Jump), consumes buffer and coyote.
  2. Airborne, falling, and will_land_within(jump_buffer_time) -> keep the buffer; the jump fires as a ground jump on the landing tick with no ground deceleration tick (A7).
  3. Otherwise airborne with air_jump_available -> DoubleJump (A6: an unused first jump past coyote becomes the double jump).
  4. Otherwise the buffer expires.
</interfaces>

## Flagged Assumptions (non-blocking; the developer may veto at review)

- **A6 (RESEARCH A6)** Walking off a ledge and pressing jump after coyote time uses the one air jump (it becomes the double jump). The first jump is not banked beyond coyote time.
- **A7** A press while falling that is predicted to land within the buffer window is kept as a buffered ground jump and does not spend the double jump, so a press "just before landing" always becomes the landing jump (D-09) and the Jump 1 -> Jump 2 chain timing (02-09) stays reliable.
- **A19** The double jump sets vertical speed to jump_speed(double_jump_height) (not additive) and keeps horizontal momentum; the release cut applies to it as to every jump (D-04).
