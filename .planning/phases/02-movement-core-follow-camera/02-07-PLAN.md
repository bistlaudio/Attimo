## Flagged Assumptions (non-blocking; the developer may veto at review)

- **A4 (RESEARCH Open Question 4)** Gym stairs use risers of at most 0.15 m; there is no step-up code. The 0.18 m theoretical limit and the 0.25 m "needs a jump" ledge are documented (CONVENTIONS by 02-02, MOVEMENT-METRICS by 02-13).
- **A14** Leaving a platform adds only its horizontal velocity; lift (upward) velocity is not carried.
- **A22** 10 TPS is a diagnostic rate (the debug_tick_rate camera-jitter toggle), not a shipped one. The 10-TPS slope runs are asserted only for "no launch (never more than 0.3 m above the highest surface of the run), reaches the far surface"; their airborne tick counts and largest gap to the surface below are printed and recorded, not pinned, because at 1.05 m per tick the 0.5 m snap cannot follow a 45 degree descent (RESEARCH probe3).
- **A23** player_controller.gd is not edited in this plan (02-05 owns it in this wave). If a scenario cannot pass with snap_min, snap_max (both only inside 0.3-0.5) or platform_carry tuning in movement_default.tres, stop and report the measured numbers as a blocker in the SUMMARY instead of committing a red test.
- **A35 (from 02-01, snap band = D-13)** The controller's speed-scaled snap is clamped to [snap_min 0.3, snap_max 0.5], D-13's band, at every tick rate, and both fields' export range is 0.3-0.5, so no tuning leaves it. At 60 TPS it gives 0.30 m at run speed, 0.31 m at the 10.5 m/s used here and is clamped to 0.5 m at dash speed. This plan proves the 60 TPS slope and stair scenarios grounded inside that band and never widens it; the band is not relaxed for the 10 TPS diagnostic rate (A22).
