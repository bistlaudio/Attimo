## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** run, sprint, jump, double jump and dash with a smooth camera, **so that** moving feels fluid and fast.

This plan adds the speed verbs: hold-to-sprint, the real ground dash, one air dash per airtime, the cooldown, the exit carry, and the dash-to-jump hand-off that 02-09 turns into the long jump.

<objective>
Add the Sprint state (D-03), replace the Phase 1 timer dash with a velocity-override dash for ground and air (D-10, D-11, D-12) gated by cooldown and the air-dash flag, combine it with the double jump in any order (D-08), and prove distance, cooldown, altitude hold, combos, exit carry and no tunnelling in test_dash. test_dash_fact is folded into test_dash.

Purpose: MOVE-02, MOVE-06 and MOVE-07 (air dash, dash into jump).
Output: sprint.gd, rewritten dash.gd, controller dash gating, Sprint node, extended test_ground_movement, new test_dash, test_dash_fact removed.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions (non-blocking; the developer may veto at review)

- **A5** Dash stays on Q and Left Ctrl until the gym sign-off (02-11).
- **A13** dash_exit_cap starts at 12 m/s. Until 02-09, a jump during a ground dash is a normal Jump; 02-09 makes it the long jump (D-06). The test here only pins "leaves Dash into a rising jump with at least run speed", so it stays valid after 02-09.
- **A26** Sprint uses ground_accel up to run speed and sprint_accel above it, so starting a sprint from standstill is never slower than starting a run.
- **A27** A jump pressed during an air dash cancels it into the double jump when that is still available (D-08 "air dash -> double jump"); otherwise the press follows the normal buffer rules after the dash.
