## Flagged Assumptions (non-blocking; the developer may veto at review)

- **A4 (RESEARCH Open Question 4)** Gym stairs use risers of at most 0.15 m; there is no step-up code. The 0.18 m theoretical limit and the 0.25 m "needs a jump" ledge are documented (CONVENTIONS by 02-02, MOVEMENT-METRICS by 02-10).
- **A14** Leaving a platform adds only its horizontal velocity; lift (upward) velocity is not carried.
- **A22** The 10-TPS slope runs are asserted only for "no launch, reaches the far surface"; their airborne tick counts are printed and recorded, not pinned, because at 1.05 m per tick a short airborne flicker is expected (RESEARCH probe3).
- **A23** player_controller.gd is not edited in this plan (02-03 owns it in this wave). If a scenario cannot pass with snap_min, snap_max or platform_carry tuning in movement_default.tres, stop and report the measured numbers as a blocker in the SUMMARY instead of committing a red test.
