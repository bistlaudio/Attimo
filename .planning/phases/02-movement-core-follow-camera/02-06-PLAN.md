## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** run, sprint, jump, double jump and dash with a smooth camera, **so that** moving feels fluid and fast.

This plan adds the measuring instruments of success criterion 5: a latency probe for FEEL-06 and a live metrics overlay for D-21, both pure listeners on the fact bus.

<objective>
Build LatencyProbe (stamps jump/dash input events, measures the time to the matching fact, records the first drawn frame after it) and MetricsOverlay (F4 overlay with per-airtime jump height and distance, dash distance, chain stage, latency summary and an F6 tick-rate cycle), prove the latency at 60 and 120 TPS headless, and put the overlay into the test scene.

Purpose: FEEL-06 needs a measured number, and tuning in the gym (D-21) needs live numbers next to the gym markings.
Output: latency_probe.gd, metrics_overlay.gd + .tscn, test_latency, test_metrics_overlay, overlay instance in test_scene.
</objective>
