## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** run, sprint, jump, double jump and dash with a smooth camera, **so that** moving feels fluid and fast.

This plan makes the tracer's CameraRig production-grade for CAM-01: no jitter at 10 TPS (and, by headless proxy, at a 144 fps frame rate; the real 144 Hz display check is a mandatory human item in 02-14), no bobbing on jumps, no clipping through walls, and the additive FOV/offset interface that Phase 3 (aim) and Phase 11 (camera kick) plug into.

<objective>
Add the D-15 vertical follow band, the D-18 additive FOV and offset sources and the pitch clamp to CameraRig, prove the interpolation-based follow is jitter-free, and prove the arm never lets a wall come between pivot and lens at 10 and 60 TPS, switching to the per-frame shape-cast fallback inside the rig if SpringArm3D fails at 10 TPS (A3).

Purpose: CAM-01 is a success criterion of the phase, and the rig interface is a costly contract for Phases 3 and 11.
Output: extended camera_rig.gd (and camera_rig.tscn only if the fallback needs node settings), test_camera_rig.gd.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions (non-blocking; the developer may veto at review)

- **A3 (RESEARCH Open Question 3)** SpringArm3D casts only on physics ticks. If it fails the 10-TPS corridor test, the rig keeps the D-18 node layout but drives the arm itself: a per-frame sphere cast in `_process` (instant pull-in, eased extension) with the SpringArm3D's own collision disabled so the two never disagree. The public interface does not change.
- **A20 (RESEARCH A2)** Querying `PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D.cast_motion` from `_process` on the main thread is allowed with default physics threading settings.
- **A21** "Never clips through walls" is tested as: no rendered frame has a World collider on the segment from pivot to lens, and the lens point is never inside a World collider.
