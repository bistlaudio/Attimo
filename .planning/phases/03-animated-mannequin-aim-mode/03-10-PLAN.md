## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have the Mannequin animate with every move and blend into an aim mode, **so that** movement reads clearly and I can aim.

This plan proves the backbone from 03-01 never makes control wait for a clip (same frame, no landing lock, 2-frame anticipation) and adds the one deliberate presentation delay the developer asked for: the air dash holds its Dodge pose past the dash (DEV-02).
