## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have the Mannequin animate with every move and blend into an aim mode, **so that** movement reads clearly and I can aim.

This plan makes aim a gameplay fact: holding the button turns the figure to the crosshair direction, keeps it there while running, jumping and dashing, and announces the change on the event bus for camera, crosshair and animation.

<objective>
Add the aim hold to PlayerController with camera-locked facing and camera-true steering, publish EventBus.aim_changed, and add the aim speed factors to MovementConfig.

Purpose: CAM-02 and D-09/D-10. Every later aim plan (camera 03-04, crosshair 03-05, pose 03-06, animation layer 03-07, arm solver 03-08) listens to this one flag and fact.
Output: aim_changed signal, PlayerController aim API, MovementConfig "Aim" group, regenerated metrics, test_aim_mode.
</objective>
