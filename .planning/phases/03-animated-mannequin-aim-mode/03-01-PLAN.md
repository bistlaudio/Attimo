## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have the Mannequin animate with every move and blend into an aim mode, **so that** movement reads clearly and I can aim.

This plan replaces the flat animation state machine with the BlendTree backbone every later plan plugs into (TimeScale, Land overlay, later Aim), removes the landing lock, records the developer decisions DEV-01 and DEV-02 (2026-11-14) and DEV-03 (2026-11-15), and ends with the decision checkpoint that settles the remaining open defaults before 03-03 builds on them. The timing guard for this backbone (same frame, no landing lock, 2-frame anticipation) and the air-dash pose hold (DEV-02) follow in 03-10.

## Developer Decisions (recorded 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15)

These are genuine developer answers given during plan revisions 1 and 2, not checkpoint defaults. The executor copies all three verbatim into 03-01-SUMMARY.md under "Confirmed decisions (03-01 checkpoint)"; later plans cite them by ID. 03-CONTEXT.md "Amendments" cross-references them.

- **DEV-01 (confirmed by the developer on 2026-11-14):** lean and pirouette go on the `Mannequin` child under `VisualRoot`, NOT on `VisualRoot` itself. ProceduralPose writes both onto the Mannequin transform inside the physics tick, pivoting about hip height. This is the developer's explicit, standalone approval of the deviation from the letter of D-01 and D-07 ("code rotation on `VisualRoot`"). Reason: `PlayerController.face()`, `Dash` and `LongJump` (direction fallback) and `CameraRig.recenter()` read VisualRoot's yaw as the facing; a 360 degree spin on VisualRoot would corrupt them mid-air. The intent of D-01/D-07 (code rotation in the visual hierarchy under the player, one parameter) is kept. Implemented by 03-06, written into docs/CONVENTIONS.md by 03-09. Resolves 03-RESEARCH.md Open Question 2.
- **DEV-02 (confirmed by the developer on 2026-11-14):** the air dash holds its Dodge pose past the Dash state's exit (D-02 "held as a pose in the air"). Documented rule: when the FSM leaves an air Dash into Fall, the animator keeps the dash node, the Dodge clip plays on at 1x and holds its last frame (it does not loop); the hold ends at the first of (a) landing, or any other state change out of Fall, which travels on that same call, or (b) the Fall transition, which the animator makes once `air_dash_hold_time` has passed since the Dash exit (start value 0.35 s, Claude's discretion: the clip's remaining 0.22 s plus a short hold of its end pose; usual jumps land inside it, long drops hand over to the Fall loop). The hold is presentation only: it never delays the FSM, steering, landing or any input (criterion 4). Implemented and guarded by 03-10; 03-07 moves the start value into MovementConfig and keeps the hold for the directional aim dashes; 03-09 documents it in docs/ANIMATION-MAP.md and checks it in the gym (item 2).
- **DEV-03 (approved by the developer on 2026-11-15, option B):** pirouette exception in aim mode, an explicit, recorded amendment of D-05 and D-07 (neither had an aim exception; written into 03-CONTEXT.md under D-05, D-07 and "Amendments"). A pirouette (Jump 3, or long jump stage 3) does not start when aim is held at takeoff. If aim is pressed, or an air dash starts, while a pirouette is running, the remaining spin finishes cleanly within `pirouette_finish_time` (start value 0.1 s, a MovementConfig Pose tunable) instead of snapping or being cut off. Implemented and tested by 03-06 (aim gate and finish cut); documented by 03-09 in docs/ANIMATION-MAP.md, docs/CONVENTIONS.md, MOVE-09 and ROADMAP Phase 3 criterion 1; judged in the 03-09 gym check (item 3). This settles the pirouette part of the former checkpoint item 3; the lean scale while aiming stays open as item 3.
