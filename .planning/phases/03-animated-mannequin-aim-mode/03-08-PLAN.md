## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** have the Mannequin animate with every move and blend into an aim mode, **so that** movement reads clearly and I can aim.

This plan makes the aiming arm point where the crosshair points, on top of the layered kit pose.

<objective>
Add an AimPoseModifier (SkeletonModifier3D) under the Mannequin skeleton that rotates spine, chest and, if needed, the upper arm so the right arm points at the camera's convergence point, fed and faded by the animator.

Purpose: D-11 ("a procedural fine adjustment rotates the aiming arm so it points at the crosshair at any camera pitch") and CAM-02. Measured arm error without correction: idle 51, run 60, strafe L 9, strafe R 114, back 56 degrees.
Output: scripts/player/aim_pose_modifier.gd, modifier node in mannequin.tscn, animator feed, test_aim_pose.
</objective>
