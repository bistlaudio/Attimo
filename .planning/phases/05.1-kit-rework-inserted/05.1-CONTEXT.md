# Phase 05.1: Kit Rework - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-24 (unattended `--auto` run: every gray area was resolved with the recommended, smallest, design-doc-faithful option; nothing here was confirmed by the user live. The audit trail is in `05.1-DISCUSSION-LOG.md`.)
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The Phase 4/04.1 mechanism kit is rebuilt as **8 logic blocks + 4 components + looks** (GAME-DESIGN.md 6.8). Every object of the three layers (steampunk, energy, nature; GAME-DESIGN.md 6.4, 6.5, 6.7) exists as block + components + a greybox look, and the coupling holds. The slingshot core plays exactly like the Loader. Targets (gear discs) replace levers, pressure plates, valves and cranks. Cut stations go, reworked ones replace their old versions, and the gym showroom is regrouped into one area per layer plus a mixed course. Requirements: KIT-01, KIT-02, KIT-03, KIT-04. Mode: MVP.

Known fixes that ride along (ROADMAP notes): the steam wall jump must feel like the normal wall jump, the wall run on it runs with the head to the wall, and the clock-hand platform has floor bugs.

Not in this phase: final looks (Group B, Phase 10), particles or juice of any kind (Group A rule), health and real damage (Phase 6), enemies, the "charge the level" gimmick (Phase 7), new player moves.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Slingshot core = the Loader you can touch (KIT-03, highest risk, built first)
- **D-01 Catch starts the hold:** entering the brass cage catches Rino, freezes the momentum and starts the hold at once. No RMB press is needed. The hold uses the Loader's own values (same slow-motion factor, same 1.2 s real-time cap, same free camera and pitch raise), read from the same config and never copied. The charge ring becomes a pressure gauge, and the dashed preview is drawn in brass. **[design detail added to GAME-DESIGN.md 6.6]**
- **D-02 Same input as the Loader:** if RMB is already held on entry, releasing it fires. If RMB is not held, a press continues the hold and the release fires (a tap fires right away). With no input, the cage auto-launches at the hold cap, the same as the Loader. **[GAME-DESIGN.md 6.6]**
- **D-03 Launch:** along the view direction at `max(entry speed, fixed push)`, with no bonus, no energy spent or gained, no grey ring and no redirect cooldown. The cage refills `loader_available`, as the slingshot already does (Phase 5 D-02). It neither refills nor spends the air dash (Phase 5 D-03 rule). Air control is locked for the Loader's release lock.
- **D-04 Shared code:** the catch & launch block drives the existing `LoaderState` / `LoaderMath` path (a source flag such as arm vs. cage) and does not get a second copy. The old mirror-throw logic in `slingshot_core.gd` is replaced. If the `Capture` state has no user left after the pneumatic tube is cut and the cannon is moved (D-05), it is removed.
- **D-05 Steam cannon = fixed-direction catch & launch:** it catches Rino with a short fixed hold (no slow motion, no input, about 0.2 s, F2-tunable) and launches along the barrel at a **fixed speed that does not depend on the entry speed**, so the arc is always the same (steampunk = predictable timing). Nothing says what the current "needs a fix" fault is, so research reproduces it at the `NozzleCannon` station before planning. **[GAME-DESIGN.md 6.6 / 6.9]**

### Known fixes (core value, built first)
- **D-06 Steam wall:** the user's "steam wall" means the wall-surface stations in the `Wand` zone (`WallRun` and the twin `WallJump` pads 4 m apart, `mechanism_rooms_builder.gd`) together with the `NozzleWall` push volume. The `NozzleWall` push is cut (GAME-DESIGN.md 6.9: "steam nozzle wall -> steam look of the wall surface"). The wall-surface block has exactly one behaviour, the existing `WallRun` / `WallJump` states with their `MovementConfig` values. A steam look is visual only and never adds an impulse.
  - Acceptance check: a side-by-side run on a plain pad and a steam-look pad gives the same exit velocity and the same facing. During the wall run Rino faces along the wall with the body upright and leans slightly toward the wall; the head never points into it.
  - Research reproduces the fling and the head-to-wall orientation (check the `ProceduralPose` roll lean sign and `face_now(_tangent)`) and checks whether the plain pad shows the same bugs.
  - Fix order (element hierarchy): station layout and surface first (for example the 4 m twin-pad spacing). Only if the plain wall jump itself is over the top, retune `wall_jump_out_speed` / `wall_jump_keep_along` / `wall_jump_control_lock` toward a conventional wall jump (mostly up, moderate push out), recorded in MOVEMENT-METRICS.md.
- **D-07 Clock-hand platform:** keep the existing bounded sweep (`RotatingPlatform` STANDING, a hand pivoted at one end, clamped below the floor angle) and make it **always on** (no receiver, no lever). A full 360-degree turn was rejected because it would invert the rider and break the "platforms never throw you off by physics" rule. This is an interpretation of "vertical rotation, always on" and is **flagged for UAT**. Research reproduces the floor bugs (carry by reference frame, floor snap, take-off chord) before the fix. The tilting platform is also always on.
- **D-08 Greatest risk first:** the first plan builds the block skeleton on exactly these items: the slingshot as Loader, the steam cannon, the wall-surface fixes and the clock-hand floor. The bulk of the new objects comes after.

### Architecture (KIT-01, GAME-DESIGN.md 6.8 guardrails)
- **D-09 Refactor, not rewrite:** proven, tuned behaviour (rotating-platform carry, crumbling, geyser, pendulum, piston wall, paternoster, boost ring, energy source, wall pad) is folded into the block scripts and keeps its tuned values. The scripts, scenes and tests of cut objects are deleted, not left dormant.
- **D-10 One script per block, one config Resource class per block** (`.tres`, like `MovementConfig`). Presets are `.tres` value sets. Looks are inherited scenes of a base block scene, grouped by layer. Folder names are the planner's call (suggestion: `scripts/kit/blocks/`, `scripts/kit/components/`, `resources/kit/`, `scenes/kit/<layer>/`).
- **D-11 No flag soup:** each block's variants are one selected profile enum (timed motion: path / sweep / tilt / swing / stamp ...; force volume: directional / radial / drag / gravity, continuous or pulsed), not combinable booleans. A variant that needs a lot of its own code becomes its own block.
- **D-12 Runtime state lives on the node.** A loaded `.tres` is never written at runtime (or it is `duplicate()`d first). The F2 panel edits node-local values.
- **D-13 Signal model:** the Trigger block replaces `SignalSource` as the emitter. The Receiver component replaces `Mechanism.source` and takes over `Behaviour` (RUN_WHILE_ON / REVERSE_ON_SIGNAL). AND / OR / timer stay as invisible logic nodes between triggers and receivers (no visible timer object).
- **D-14 The 4 components, each with two real users** (this satisfies both KIT-01 and guardrail 3):
  - Receiver: switched speed pad, drawbridge, gates.
  - Damage / push: pendulum, steam hammer, piston wall, snap bloom, falling debris.
  - Amplification: bounce pad with core, gravity zone.
  - Energy cost: fast escape from the attraction field, and **energy core pay-in** (D-20). **[GAME-DESIGN.md 6.8]**
- **D-15 Damage before health exists:** the damage / push component pushes now (knockback, as pendulum and piston wall do today) and emits a value-carrying fact (for example `player_hit(amount, position, source_id)`). Health and real damage consume that fact in Phase 6. There is no death from kit damage in this phase; fall kill zones still respawn as before. **[GAME-DESIGN.md 6.8]**
- **D-16 Tests:** every block has a contract test of its interface (exported config, incoming and outgoing signals), and every object has a smoke test. One **coupling test** walks every kit object scene: each root exports a layer tag (steampunk / energy / nature) and an energy-relation tag (free / costs / amplified) as plain data. Costs or amplified requires a turquoise `GlowElement`. Nature never carries an Impulse or Catch & launch block or an amplification or energy-cost component. Steampunk never carries an energy-cost component.
- **D-17 Mechanisms still influence the player only through the `PlayerController` request APIs** (04.1 D-06), and EventBus facts carry values (ids, positions, amounts), never node references.

### Objects and layer rules (KIT-02)
- **D-18 Object list = GAME-DESIGN.md 6.4 / 6.5 / 6.7 exactly**, with no additions. New objects in this phase: rail cart, piston steps, drawbridge, steam hammer, steam stream, speed pad, target, charge gate, force-field barrier, attraction-field trap, sinking debris, tipping trunk, leaf storm, resin pool, collapse, falling debris, snap bloom.
- **D-19 Target = gear disc:** a plate-like brass gear rim with a turquoise core. It is a Trigger block that fires on any shot hit (tap or charged) and gets the Phase 5 snap assist. A shot is the only way to fire it, with no free trigger. Every target station keeps an energy capsule with respawn next to it (Phase 5 D-18 / D-24 carried forward).
- **D-20 Energy core pay-in = hold E near the core:** energy drains at a steady rate through the energy-cost component, the player stops paying when they let go, and the paid amount persists on the node. The output is stepwise: each paid threshold (start at 25 / 50 / 75, F2) switches one more receiver (more parkour spawns or a farther door). E's remaining jobs are this pay-in and the existing interact prompt. **[GAME-DESIGN.md 6.4]**
- **D-21 Charge gate:** open while the player is inside its check volume with energy >= its threshold. It never consumes energy, and it shows the threshold as core marks in turquoise.
- **D-22 Force-field barrier:** breaks only to a Loader discharge **with bonus** (a reaction to load on impact). A plain redirect without bonus does not break it. In the gym it restores after `respawn_time`.
- **D-23 Attraction-field trap:** inside its radius it pulls toward the core. A slow escape (walking or running out against the pull) is always possible and free, so it is never a hard trap. A fast escape (dash or Loader out of the field) suspends the pull and drains 20/s through the energy-cost component while it lasts. The exact trigger of the "fast" case is the planner's call within these constraints.
- **D-24 Amplification** reads the arm charge and never consumes it. The gravity zone floats longer with more charge, and the bounce pad with core bounces higher and further with more charge. The plain bounce pad (steampunk, free) is its base version.
- **D-25 Speed pad replaces conveyors:** an Impulse block giving a short forward boost (the free counterpart of the dash hop), in a steam boost-nozzle look. At least one in the gym is switched by a target.
- **D-26 Greybox looks per layer:** steampunk keeps the `MechanismLook` brass / copper / iron pass, and energy adds a turquoise `GlowElement` core on a brass body. Nature gets a sibling look pass with a small nature palette (moss green, bark brown, amber resin, weathered stone) on Prototype Bits pieces. There are no particles (Group A rule): the leaf storm reads through simple animated mesh strips or streamers, the resin pool through an amber floor plane, and steam through static or animated greybox columns as today.
- **D-27 Nature never helps:** the leaf storm is placed to push against the intended route, and the resin pool is a drag zone (less speed, so less passive charge). Nature objects never touch energy.

### Showroom and gym (KIT-04, ROADMAP criteria 4 and 5)
- **D-28 The showroom is the gym's mechanism stations.** The code shows the "showcase stations" (`mechanism_rooms_builder.gd`, `build_showcase_stations`, `test_showcase_layout.gd`) live inside the movement gym. Criterion 4 (regrouped by layer and role, a station and smoke test per object) is met by the per-object stations inside three new **layer areas** (steampunk, energy, nature), each grouped by role (platforms, environment, obstacles, boosters, mechanisms). Criterion 5 is met by those areas plus a **mixed course** that combines the layers. Every station has an "Erwartet:" sign.
- **D-29 Builder-only:** the mechanism rooms and the Phase 4 puzzle rooms (`mechanism_rooms_builder.gd`, `puzzle_rooms_builder.gd`) are replaced by kit builders (one per layer area plus the mixed course, or one kit builder; planner's call). The gym is never hand-edited. Puzzle rooms that depend on cut triggers (lever, valve, crank, pressure plate, lift, tube) are removed. Their logic ideas (AND / OR / timer) return as target-based logic stations in the energy area and in the mixed course.
- **D-30 Phase 5 UAT protection:** 13 of the 14 pending checks in `05-HUMAN-UAT.md` run in `Zone_Loader`, `Zone_Energy`, the shot stations, the HUD and the arm. These stay **untouched** (same positions, same stations). UAT item 11 (west-wing "Erwartet:" walkthrough and the 04.1 re-check of the Auslöser stations and puzzle rooms) covers stations this phase replaces. The user can still run it on the pre-05.1 state, commit `d51389e`; after 05.1 the new stations carry their own "Erwartet:" checks. Do not edit `05-HUMAN-UAT.md`.
- **D-31 Mixed course:** one short route that combines all three layers (for example clock hand -> slingshot -> resin pool -> target-switched drawbridge -> collapse), built against MOVEMENT-METRICS.md (jump, dash-hop and Loader distances plus a margin) and walked at real speed.
- **D-32 Teach / test / twist:** gym stations only in this phase. The real beats come in the Phase 6 teaching rooms and the Phase 7 level.

### Scope and plans
- **D-33** Stay in the GSD band of 3-5 plans (Phase 4 D-30), written as MVP vertical slices. Wave 1 is D-08 (the riskiest items) on the new block skeleton.
