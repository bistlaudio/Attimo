# Phase 05.1 Plan 21: Nature layer on Reaction to load Summary

The nature objects are profiles of the Reaction to load block: CRUMBLE (platform and 6 segment collapse strip), SINK, TIP, DROP and BITE, each with a preset and a nature look. Falling debris and snap bloom push through DamagePush and announce one `player_hit`. Nothing in the block touches energy. The old crumbling platform script, scene and test are gone.
