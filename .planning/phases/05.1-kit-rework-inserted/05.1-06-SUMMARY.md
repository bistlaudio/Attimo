# Phase 05.1 Plan 06: Gym Cutover to the Kit Layer Areas Summary

**The showroom behind the hall's east door is now the kit: three layer areas (steampunk, energy layer, nature) line one corridor, each with its role groups and a header, tint, walls and its own fall reset, built from a shared shell. The seven steampunk stations of plans 01-04 (clock hand, tilt plate, wall-run pair, wall-jump twin, steam cannon, slingshot) moved into Zone_KitSteampunk with their tuned offsets and signs. The old mechanism rooms, their builder and tests, and the cut lift, crank and pressure plate are gone. A new structural test holds every later plan to "one signed station per kit object in its layer area".**
