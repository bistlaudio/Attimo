# Phase 05.1 Plan 13: Loader field, Zone_Energy swap and lever/valve/gate cut Summary

The Loader field is the first profile of the new Reaction to load block: a turquoise field that never breaks, lets a straight Loader flight with at least 2 paid steps glide through with a one-time 30 % brake and a ripple, and softly pushes back everything else (walk, dash, 1-step Loader, slingshot flight). The two former shot stations in Zone_Energy now hold this field at their old positions, and Lever, Valve and GearGate are gone.
