# Phase 05.1 Plan 03: Wall Surface Block and the Two Wall Fixes Summary

**The wall pad is now the always-on Wall surface kit block with a plain brass and a steam look for both the wall-run (4 x 12 m) and the wall-jump (8 x 12 m) surface. Plain and steam looks give bit-identical exit velocities and facing. The wall-run lean is clamped by head clearance in ProceduralPose, so the head bone stays at least 0.45 m from the wall (closest 0.452 m, lean 4.38 degrees instead of 18). The wall-jump twin stands 8 m tall with 6 m between the faces, so a held kick is caught by the far pad instead of flying over it. The nozzle wall and jump modes and the old WallPad are gone.**

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - consequence of unattended decision 2] test_wall_states lean check relaxed from `> 0.1` to `> 0.0`**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** `_scenario_lean` asked for `up . -normal > 0.1` (a lean above 5.7 degrees); the geometric head clamp gives 4.38 degrees (0.076).
- **Fix:** the check now asks only for a tilt toward the wall, as the plan's own must-have does (up . wall normal < 0); the clamp itself was not loosened.

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] test_kit_blocks BLOCKS spec: optional `profile_enum` / `profile_field`**
- The contract test hard-coded the enum name `Profile` and the field `profile`; the plan names the wall enum `Surface` (field `surface`). Added optional keys (default `Profile` / `profile`) instead of renaming the plan's enum. WallSurface contract: probe `height`, positive fields `height`, `width`, presets per surface.
