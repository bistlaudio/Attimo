<objective>
Rebuild the Loader as a paid, straight-line discharge (D-48..D-50): press pays step 1, holding loads up to 3 steps (one per 0.3 s real time), the user's speed formula with the 22 m/s cap and the overflow-to-distance rule, and a gravity-free straight flight owned by the PlayerController. The slingshot cage stays free and flies the same line. The bus fact `loader_released` carries `steps: int` instead of `bonus: bool`.

Purpose: ENRG-09 (paid hold, formula, straight flight, overflow) and ENRG-07 (the Loader refuses at 0 steps); KIT-03 (the cage still plays like the Loader on the new line). This is the core-value risk of the replan, so it comes right after the clock (D-58).
Output: new LoaderState flow, the cage as a free 2-step Loader flight (D-60), LoaderMath functions, straight flight in the controller, Loader MovementConfig fields, deleted trajectory preview (its input API is gone; the segmented ring follows in 05.1-12), Loader metric rows in steps.

Seam with 05.1-12 (decided): this plan keeps `loader_min_push`, `loader_bonus_speed` and `LoaderMath.trajectory / step / flat_reach / apex_height` ONLY because `tools/loader_room_builder.gd` (l.86-89, 206-207) and `test_loader_room` still read them; 05.1-12 re-derives the Zone_Loader sizes from the new straight reach and deletes them. No other code may read them after this plan. The trajectory preview (`LoaderPreview`) is deleted here because it is driven by the removed bonus API (`preview_velocity_for(with_bonus)`), which also fulfils the "no trajectory line" part of D-51.
</objective>
