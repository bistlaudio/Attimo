# Phase 05.1 Plan 04: Timed Motion Block (Clock Hand, Tilt Plate) and the Floor Fixes Summary

**The rotating platform is now the always-on Timed motion kit block with two profiles: the brass clock hand (SWEEP) and the iron tilt plate (TILT). Each station now stands at `TimedMotion.min_pivot_height`, so the swept deck no longer dips through the floor. With a real floor, a rider 7 m out on the hand and 3.5 m out on the plate stands on the deck on every one of 599 sampled ticks and never touches the static floor. The worst feet offset is 0.090 m on the hand and 0.067 m on the plate. At the old 1.0 m pivot the same riders were left up to 3.15 m off the deck. A jump off a rising deck now keeps the deck's vertical speed: 11.095 + 1.671 = 12.767 m/s, measured. Static and paternoster jumps are unchanged at 11.0952 m/s. The old RotatingPlatform and the LYING gear are gone.**

## Accomplishments

- `TimedMotionConfig` has `enum Profile { SWEEP, TILT }` and `enum Timing { ALWAYS_ON, SWITCHED }` (D-11). The motion values (angular_speed, ramp_time, sweep_limit_deg, tilt_limit_deg) and the deck sizes (hand_length, hand_width, plate_size, plate_thickness) keep the old defaults, and every field has a positive range minimum.
- `TimedMotion` (extends KitBlock) copies the carry contract unchanged (D-09): an AnimatableBody3D `Body` as a direct child, `sync_to_physics` off, group `carrying_platform`, physics priority 100, the `carry_velocity_at` chord, a floor-angle clamp of minus 10 degrees, and the eased ramp. ALWAYS_ON targets 1.0 from the first tick (D-07). SWITCHED is declared and reads a child `Receiver` (`is_on()`) when one is present. It also adds `FLOOR_CLEARANCE = 0.3` and the statics `deck_reach`, `limit_deg`, `deck_drop`, `lowest_deck_y(config, pivot_y)` and `min_pivot_height(config, floor_top)`. These use the raw config limit, which is conservative because the runtime clamp can only lower it.
- Looks: `clock_hand_platform.tscn` has a brass hand, an iron axle housing and brass caps. `tilting_platform.tscn` has an iron plate, a brass axle and caps. Both are STEAMPUNK and FREE with no GlowElement; the old platform's turquoise glow is dropped because turquoise means energy.
- PlayerController: `_leave_floor` adds `clampf(_last_platform_velocity.y, -6, 6)` to `vertical_speed` when the floor it left was a reference-frame carrier. This runs after the coyote check and only for finite values. New constant `MAX_DECK_TAKEOFF_SPEED = 6.0`; MovementConfig is unchanged.
- Gym (Tragen): the `RotatingStanding` (clock hand, pivot 5.39 m) and `RotatingTilting` (tilt plate, pivot 2.80 m) stations keep their slots. Both are kind `kit` with no lever. The signs read "Uhrzeiger-Plattform" / "Kipp-Plattform" with "Erwartet: Läuft immer, schwingt hin und her, du bleibst auf dem Deck".
  - The clock hand has a ledge of four tile columns (tops 5.0 / 3.75 / 2.5 / 1.25) that steps west into the freed slot (0,0). The top step ends 0.5 m behind the pivot end.
  - The tilt plate has two columns (2.5 / 1.25) west of its axis edge, which is the fixed point.
  - `RotatingLying` is removed.
- `rotating_platform.gd`, its `.uid` and `rotating_platform.tscn` are deleted. The gym is regenerated (`GYM: WRITTEN`). The first tool run logged two "Failed loading resource rotating_platform" lines because it loaded the stale gym before rewriting it; the second run was clean, as in plan 02.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Ray-guard body floor 12 -> 11 in test_showcase_layout**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** The cut LYING gear was one of the moving bodies the ray guard counts.
- **Fix:** The floor is now 11. The clock hand and tilt plate are counted as `TimedMotion` bodies.

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] test_energy_gym "Erwartet:" floor 46 -> 45** (the RotatingLying sign is gone). The file was not in the plan's list.

**3. [Test design] Priority-50 probe for the per-tick checks (`Fixtures.spawn_physics_probe`)**
- The deck ticks after the player (priority 100). Sampling after `physics_frame` would mix the rider of tick N with the deck pose of tick N, which adds about 0.12 m of pure lag at 7 m. The probe samples after the player has moved and before the deck moves.
- The probe is a Node with a runtime GDScript, so no extra file was needed.

**4. [Test design] Take-off rider at 4 m, not 7 m**
- At 7 m the deck chord reaches about 7 m/s, which is above the 6 m/s take-off clamp. The take-off scenario stands 4 m out (chord at take-off 1.67 m/s), and the expectation uses `static + clamp(chord)`.
- The static value is measured in the same run at the same point (the first airborne tick), as the plan asks. It is 11.095 m/s, not research's 6.81: a different measurement point in the research probe, not re-checked.

**5. [Test design] Positive control** at the old pivot 1.0 m for both profiles. It must touch the static floor at least once, which proves the per-tick "never the floor" check can fail.

**6. [Design] Deck sizes are config fields** (hand_length, hand_width, plate_size, plate_thickness), not block constants, so `deck_drop` / `min_pivot_height` read everything from the config. The defaults are the old constants.

**7. [Design] `ramp_time` range minimum 0.0 -> 0.05** (T-05.1-13 strictly positive). The default of 0.3 is unchanged, and the runtime `maxf(ramp_time, 0.01)` guard stays.

**8. [Layout] Gym tags kept** as `RotatingStanding` / `RotatingTilting`, as the plan asks ("at the same slots"). The row key `deck_preset` resolves the pivot height in `_place_station`, because STATIONS is a const. Ledge steps are 1.25 m, which is under DECK_STEP_MAX 1.5 and well under the 2.0 m held plain jump. The top step sits 0.39 m (hand) / 0.30 m (plate) under the deck, so it is never coplanar with it.

**9. [Scope] test_moving_mechanisms:** the REVERSE_ON_SIGNAL scenario is removed, because the kit block is always on and the lever reversal no longer exists. The layer check now uses the clock hand's body; the lift scenarios are unchanged.

**10. [Docs] docs/CONVENTIONS.md** `carrying_platform` row now names the Timed motion decks and the vertical take-off chord.

**11. [Plan no-op] test_mechanism_rooms.gd** was in Task 3's file list but has no rotating-platform reference; no edit was needed (green in the filtered and full runs).

**12. [Tooling] gsd-sdk state handlers:** `state.record-metric` needed flags instead of positional args, and `state.record-session` wrote a stale `stopped_at`; STATE.md position, stopped_at and the three decisions were fixed by hand.
