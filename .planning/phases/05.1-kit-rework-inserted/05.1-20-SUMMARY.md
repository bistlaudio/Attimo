# Phase 05.1 Plan 20: Pendulum, piston wall and steam hammer with DamagePush Summary

The steampunk obstacles run on the Timed motion block (SWING and STAMP profiles) with the new DamagePush component, which knocks the player back through `knock_back` and announces `player_hit` with values only. The old pendulum and piston-wall scripts, scenes and tests are gone.
