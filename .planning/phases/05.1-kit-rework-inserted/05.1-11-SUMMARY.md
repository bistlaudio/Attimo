# Phase 05.1 Plan 11: Paid Loader and straight flight Summary

The Loader is now a paid, straight-line discharge: a press pays step 1, holding pays up to 3 steps in real time, the release flies a gravity-free line of 6, 10 or 14 m plus the overflow of the 22 m/s cap, and the slingshot cage flies the same line for free as a 2-step Loader.

## Tasks

1. RED tests for the paid hold, the formula, the straight flight, the tunnelling case and the cage: test commit e370dea.
2. LoaderState, LoaderMath, straight flight in the controller, config fields, bus fact and cage: feat commit.
3. Trajectory preview deleted, crosshair and look on the new API, Loader metric rows per step: feat commit.

## Key results

- Measured (docs/MOVEMENT-METRICS.md): 1 step standing 6.0 m/s, 6.0 m straight; 3 steps standing 10.0 m/s, 14.1 m straight; 3 steps from a run and in a dash hop hit the 22 m/s cap, the hop flies 20.7 m straight (overflow); slingshot from standing 8.0 m/s, 10.05 m straight, 12.5 m reach.
- Thin-wall tunnelling (0.2 m) at 22 m/s passes at 60 and at 10 TPS (swept move_and_slide).
- Removed: loader_cooldown_timer, loader_redirect_cooldown, loader_bonus_cost, bonus_ready, preview_velocity(_for), LoaderPreview.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] LoaderMath.legacy_speed**
- The builder and test_loader_room still compute the old speed; the new 6-argument discharge_speed broke them. A small legacy helper keeps them compiling until 05.1-12.

**2. [Rule 1 - Stale assertion] test_loader_room live cases**
- Gap "without a core" is now an empty clock (the press is refused, the player stays on the platform); the ledge "with a core" asserts the straight 3-step discharge rises above the ledge instead of landing on it. Zone_Loader geometry is 05.1-12's job.

**3. [Rule 1 - Stale assertion] Ground-throw ledge test**
- The throw is slower than the old bonus throw, so the scenario now runs up on a longer floor and holds 3 steps so the throw still carries Rino over the ledge above run speed.

**4. [Test robustness] RED commit has parse errors**
- The RED tests call the new typed signatures directly, so the RED state is a parse failure naming discharge_speed, discharge_speed_uncapped and the new fields (not the reflection style the plan suggested).

**5. LoaderProfile.cage() takes no argument; LoaderLook owns BRASS_COLOR**
- Both as planned; listed because several tests and builders changed with them.
