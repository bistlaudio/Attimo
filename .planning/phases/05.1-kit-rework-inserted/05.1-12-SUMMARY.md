# Phase 05.1 Plan 12: Segmented ring, 89 degree camera and straight-reach Zone_Loader Summary

The Loader ring is now 3 step segments at 0.6 scale, the crosshair shrinks while holding or caged, the camera looks up to 89 degrees everywhere, and Zone_Loader keeps every station position while its gap (13 m) and ledge (11 m) are re-derived from the 1-step vs 3-step straight Loader.

## Tasks

1. RED tests: commit 4856a22, parse and assertion failures naming LOADER_SEGMENTS, crosshair_scale, pitch 89, launch_reach.
2. Ring, gauge, crosshair scale and camera: 3f89e25.
3. Zone_Loader re-sizing, deletions, metrics and gym regeneration: e197348.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Ledge tilt**
- Found during: Task 3 live test. The 15 degree tilt toward the ledge made the capsule clip the ledge face (peak 10.8, fell back). Tilt set to 11 degrees; player ends on the ledge (x -59.1, y 11.0).

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing] Lens inside the capsule at the pole**
- The floor shortens the arm so the lens sat under Rino inside the capsule footprint. Added `_clear_player_capsule` (D-52 requirement "never inside the player capsule").

**3. Gap reach includes the ballistic tail**
- The plan says "straight_reach"; the straight line ends after 6 or 14 m and gravity resumes, so the real landing reach is the line plus the tail. `LoaderMath.launch_reach` and `launch_apex` build on `straight_reach` for the line; the margins (1 m gap, 0.5 m ledge) are asserted against them and verified live (3 steps cross, 1 step does not; 3 steps reach the ledge, 1 step peaks at 6.4 m).

**4. LoaderLook unchanged**
- It had no pitch-raise code left (that lived in CameraRig); only the old pitch test scenario was removed.

## Pre-existing note
