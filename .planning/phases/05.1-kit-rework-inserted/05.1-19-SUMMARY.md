## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Design gap] Extra config fields.** The plan interface lists `path_points`, `path_speed`, `path_pause` and the `step_*` fields. PATH also needs a deck count (`path_deck_count`, the old paternoster had 4 cabins) and STEPS needs the row geometry (`step_pitch`, `step_rise`); deck size reuses `plate_size` and `plate_thickness`.

**2. [Rule 1 - Test scope] Piston steps rider check counts only the first retraction.** After the rider fell off (allowed once the step is retracted) the next extension cycle made the watcher count him as missing. The assert now stops counting once the step dropped under 1.1 m.

**3. [Rule 1 - Stale assertions] test_look_pass (scene count 9 to 8) and test_movement_gym (3 to 5 Timed motion profiles)** changed because the paternoster scene left `scenes/mechanisms` and the gym gained PATH and STEPS blocks.

**4. [Discretion] Paternoster is always on** (the plan says all are always on); the old run-while-on trigger is gone. GAME-DESIGN.md (platforms row), both HTML views and the change log record it.

**5. Station placement.** All three stand in the north far row (row 0): rail cart at (1, 0), paternoster at (6, 0), piston steps at (7, 0); slots (0, 0) and (2, 0) stay empty for the cart's ledges.
