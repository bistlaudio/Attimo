## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Spec mismatch] Timer measures from the rising edge**
- The plan says the pad "stops boosting 5 s after the gate went inactive". LogicTimer (unchanged, per its contract) starts its hold on the gate's rising edge, so the pad stops hold_time after the player entered. test_charge_gate asserts exactly that (on at the edge, off after about 5 s).

**2. [Rule 1 - Design correction] Bridge stations go through a 10 s timer**
- Plan text says the gate "drives the bridge". With RUN_WHILE_ON and a direct link the bridge would fold up the moment the player leaves the arch, so no one could cross. The station uses gate -> LogicTimer(10 s) -> bridge; the direct link (gate -> receiver) is covered by a test of its own.

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Receiver wiring inside an instanced scene**
- A Receiver given the gym as owner was saved as a second, new node next to the instanced one. The builder helper `KitAreaShell.wire_receiver` now marks the block `set_editable_instance` and keeps the instance owner, which saves a real override (`source = NodePath("../../Timer")`); test_kit_gym_layout checks that every Receiver in the three stations has a source.

**4. [Rule 1 - Stale assertion] test_movement_gym expected 2 Timed motion profiles; now 3 (SWEEP, TILT, HINGE).** No design value changed.
