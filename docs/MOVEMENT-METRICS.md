# Movement Metrics

Generated by tools/measure_movement.gd on 2026-11-27 with Godot 4.7.2-stable (official). Regenerate with `bash attimo/tests/run.sh --tool res://tools/measure_movement.gd`.

Every number comes from the real Player driven headless with scripted per-tick inputs at 60 TPS on flat ground. A distance is take-off to landing of the body origin (the feet), a height is the apex above the take-off point, times have tick resolution (1/60 s). The gym markings (02-14) are the manual cross-check. If movement_default.tres is retuned, regenerate this file: test_movement_metrics fails on a stale config snapshot.

## Long jump (dash hop)

The long jump is the dash hop: a jump during a ground dash or within dash_jump_grace after it. Its speed is set per stage (long_jump_speed_1/2/3), never carried from the dash. Stage 1 is the first hop; each clean repeat (a ground dash started inside the landing window after touchdown, then a jump) climbs to stage 2 and 3; the combo ends after stage 3 and the next hop starts again at stage 1; a miss (window runs out, standing still, a normal jump in between) restarts at stage 1. The speeds are capped by an export maximum of 22 m/s, the envelope the camera and tunnelling tests cover. Take-off speed is the horizontal speed on the first tick of the long jump. One hop streak is measured: dash + jump on the landing tick of each hop.

## Loader

The right-click Loader (HAND-04, ENRG-09): a press pays step 1, holding pays one more step every loader_step_interval of real time up to loader_max_steps; the release throws along the view at v + push + n * (push_step + k * v), capped at loader_max_speed, and flies a straight gravity-free line of loader_flight_distance[n] plus the overflow (the speed the cap cut, times loader_overflow_gain) before gravity resumes. Measured with the real Player and 12 steps on the clock, debug_loader_direction (no camera): ground rows aim 10 degrees above the horizon, the hop row and the up row are as named, 10 ticks of hold for 1 step and 40 for 3. Distance is the straight flight from the release point to its end, reach the horizontal distance to the landing. The slingshot cage is a free 2-step flight along the same line. The gym room Zone_Loader is sized from these numbers (D-59): its gap lies between the 1-step and the 3-step landing reach, its ledge between the 1-step and the 3-step apex, both from a standing start along the station direction.

## Energy

The arm energy is a clock of 12 whole steps (D-34, ENRG-06 to ENRG-08, FEEL-10): every cost and income is a whole number of steps and time appears only as tick intervals. The tick rows run ArmEnergy.tick until the next step; the capsule and machine rows take their steps through EnergySource.take_step (the Extractor moves one step per interval); the cost rows are the config values; the Moto rows run the real Player with a full arm. ## Latency

Software latency from the input event (stamped in _input by LatencyProbe) to the gameplay fact on the EventBus: jump press to jumped, dash press to dash_started. 10 presses per cell, spread over different points of the physics tick. Each cell shows the median and the maximum in ms, with Input.use_accumulated_input on (the default) and off (RESEARCH A9). Targets: median <= 66 ms, every sample <= 100 ms (FEEL-06).
