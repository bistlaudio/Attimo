# Movement Metrics

Generated by tools/measure_movement.gd on 2026-11-05 with Godot 4.7.2-stable (official). Regenerate with `bash attimo/tests/run.sh --tool res://tools/measure_movement.gd`.

Every number comes from the real Player driven headless with scripted per-tick inputs at 60 TPS on flat ground. A distance is take-off to landing of the body origin (the feet), a height is the apex above the take-off point, times have tick resolution (1/60 s). The gym markings (02-14) are the manual cross-check. If movement_default.tres is retuned, regenerate this file: test_movement_metrics fails on a stale config snapshot.

## Latency

Software latency from the input event (stamped in _input by LatencyProbe) to the gameplay fact on the EventBus: jump press to jumped, dash press to dash_started. 10 presses per cell, spread over different points of the physics tick. Each cell shows the median and the maximum in ms, with Input.use_accumulated_input on (the default) and off (RESEARCH A9). Targets: median <= 66 ms, every sample <= 100 ms (FEEL-06).

Display, compositor and USB latency are outside the software measurement (D-22). End-to-end hardware latency stays a flagged human item in the 02-14 sign-off.
