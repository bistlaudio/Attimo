Essential experience

Feel like a perpetual motion machine that runs on time: every ability costs a moment from your clock, the steampunk world winds it back up, and when you stop time, aim and launch, you choose how many moments to spend.

Attimo means "the moment": the rush of never slowing down, and the calm control of the frozen second in which you decide where all that speed goes.

Pillars

Four pillars

1

Energy is time

Rino's arm is a clock of 12 steps. Every ability costs whole steps; running and the jump chain stay free.

2

Freeze the moment

Hold to freeze your momentum in slow motion, load 1–3 steps, release to fly a straight line where you aim.

3

The world winds you up

Steampunk machines move you for free and tick the clock forward. Moves that cost steps never charge.

4

Read the world at a glance

Brass helps if you hit its rhythm, turquoise asks you to spend, nature tries to slow you down.

System view

What drives the loop, what holds it back

Reinforcing

  • Riding steampunk boosters → +1 step → paid abilities → reach more boosters
  • Moto Perpetuo from 6 steps makes running faster and Jump 3 higher
  • Opened routes lead to new capsules, machines and enemies

Balancing

  • Every ability costs whole steps; paid moves never charge
  • Hard cap at 12; respawn always with 3 steps
  • Health apart: 3–5 points, i-frames ~1 s and knockback after a hit
  • Passive tick is deliberately slow (+1 / 20 s)
  • Nature slows you down (resin pool, leaf storm) and never helps
  • Extracting leaves you standing and vulnerable
Scope & anti-scope

Not a combat game, not an open world, not a story game. One level of pure momentum, beaten by moving well.

In scope

  • Run, triple jump, dash, dash hop, wall run
  • Energy clock (12 steps), 4 cores, Moto Perpetuo
  • Loader: slow motion, 1–3 steps, straight line
  • Extractor with beam
  • Mechanism kit in 3 themed layers
  • 1–2 enemy types, health with i-frames, one level to the lab

Nice to have

  • More looks per logic block
  • More nature objects
  • Clock face on the arm, another module
  • Collectibles
  • Ledge grab

Out of scope

  • Multiple levels
  • Dialogue and cutscenes
  • Shooting, full combat system
  • Pull/push, old aim mode
  • Skill trees, save slots
  • Custom animation authoring

Theme check

Why the energy is a clock

  • Attimo = "the moment"; moment and momentum share one root: energy is time, every ability costs a moment
  • Clockwork is one of the three themes (clock-hand platform, gears)
  • Moto Perpetuo is the perpetual-motion idea of clockwork
  • The Loader stretches the moment in slow motion
  • 12 = 4 cores × 3: every arm core is a quarter of the face
  • A clock keeps ticking by itself: the small passive income
Right click · paid in steps

The Loader

  1. Hold · momentum freezes, slow motion ~0.25×, free camera; the ring loads 1 step after another (max 3), each paid at once
  2. Look · smaller ring with 3 step segments, no trajectory line, turquoise vignette
  3. Release · straight line, no parabola: own + push + steps × (push step + k × own), overflow above the cap = longer straight flight
  4. Empty clock · does not fire (klack, red flash)
  5. Rules · ground: hard brake, then launch (also up) · once per air phase · air dash untouched
  6. Camera · up to ~89° everywhere, never flips backward
  7. Feel · "geladen": a loaded spring, sharp release with a short hitstop
Left click

The Extractor

Extractor · hold

Cone in front of the hand with a turquoise beam to the source. Capsule +3, broken machine +5; then it goes dark and refills. Enemies follow in Phase 6 (drain, plus Loader hits).

Shot · removed

Shot, charge ring and gear-disc targets are gone. The module system stays dormant for a later module; the mouse wheel switches nothing for now.

Mechanism kit

Three layers, three questions

Steampunk

Timing

"Can you hit the rhythm?" Free, mechanical, helps you.

Energy

Decision

"Do you spend it?" Costs or amplified, always turquoise.

Nature

Resistance

"Can you keep your speed?" World and danger, never helps.

Platforms are neutral. Every ability has a free counterpart in the world, and each steampunk booster adds +1 step. Capsules and broken machines are extracted. A mixed course in the gym runs through all three layers in one route.

Teaching the Loader without a tutorial

Slingshot core

  1. Enter · a small brass cage catches Rino only when he is inside it
  2. Hold · same slow motion as the Loader, smaller pressure gauge, no line
  3. Release · steam blast, straight launch where you look as a free 2-step Loader (10 m line + overflow), +1 step

Same input and timing as the Loader: entering starts the hold, releasing RMB (or a tap) fires, otherwise auto-launch at the Loader cap. Only the look says "free and mechanical".
