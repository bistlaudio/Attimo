Essential experience

Feel like a perpetual motion machine: every bit of speed charges your arm, and every charge throws you faster through an overgrown steampunk ruin, until you stop time, aim, and launch.

Attimo means "the moment": the rush of never slowing down, and the calm control of the frozen second in which you decide where all that speed goes.

Pillars

Four pillars

1

Momentum is power

Speed charges Rino's arm, energy buys more speed. Every move feeds one loop.

2

Freeze the moment

Hold to freeze your momentum in slow motion, look around, release to discharge it where you aim.

3

Free base, charged power

Every move works without energy. Energy makes it higher, longer, further. Never a failed moment.

4

Read the world at a glance

Brass helps if you hit its rhythm, turquoise asks you to spend, nature tries to slow you down.

The energy economy

System view

What drives the loop, what holds it back

Reinforcing

  • More speed → more passive charge → more speed
  • Moto Perpetuo at 50 makes running faster, which charges faster
  • Opened routes lead to new capsules and enemies

Balancing

  • Injected moves and the Loader cost energy
  • Hard cap at 100; respawn always with one core
  • Nature slows you down (resin pool, leaf storm), so you charge less
  • Extracting is efficient but slow and leaves you vulnerable
Scope & anti-scope

Not a combat game, not an open world, not a story game. One level of pure momentum, beaten by moving well.

In scope

  • Run, triple jump, dash, dash hop, wall run
  • Energy arm, 4 cores, Moto Perpetuo
  • Loader with slow motion and trajectory
  • Extractor and Shot modules
  • Mechanism kit in 3 themed layers
  • 1–2 enemy types, one level, one boss

Nice to have

  • More looks per logic block
  • More nature objects
  • Third hand module
  • Collectibles
  • Ledge grab

Out of scope

  • Multiple levels
  • Dialogue and cutscenes
  • Full combat system
  • Pull/push, old aim mode
  • Skill trees, save slots
  • Custom animation authoring

The Loader

  1. Hold · momentum freezes, slow motion ~0.25×, free camera, max ~1.2 s
  2. Look · charge ring fills with the stored speed, dashed trajectory, turquoise vignette
  3. Release · discharge in the view direction: max(stored, min push) + bonus
  4. Empty arm · redirect only, grey ring, cooldown
  5. Rules · ground: hard brake, then launch (also up) · once per air phase · air dash untouched
  6. Feel · "geladen": a loaded spring, sharp release with a short hitstop
Left click · mouse wheel switches

Two modules

Extractor · hold

Cone in front of the hand. Drains enemies, shards, capsules and broken machines. Efficient, slow, risky.

Shot · tap or hold

A projectile, never a beam (Mega Man / Samus). Tap 10, hold charges up to 25 with a full-charge tell. Strong snap to gear-disc targets; enemies burst into shards.

Mechanism kit

Three layers, three questions

Steampunk

Timing

"Can you hit the rhythm?" Free, mechanical, helps you.

Energy

Decision

"Do you spend it?" Costs or amplified, always turquoise.

Nature

Resistance

"Can you keep your speed?" World and danger, never helps.

Platforms are neutral. Every energy move has a free counterpart in the world. The only energy source is extracting capsules and broken machines.

Teaching the Loader without a tutorial

Slingshot core

  1. Enter · a brass cage catches Rino with a steam hiss
  2. Hold · same slow motion as the Loader, pressure gauge instead of the ring
  3. Release · steam blast, launch where you look, no energy bonus

Same input and timing as the Loader; only the look says "free and mechanical".
