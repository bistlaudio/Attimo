Essential experience

Feel like a perpetual motion machine that runs on time: every ability costs a moment from the clock on your arm, the steampunk world winds it back up, and when you stop time, aim and launch, you choose how many moments to spend.

Attimo means "the moment", and moment and momentum share one root. The player should leave with two feelings: the rush of never slowing down, and the calm control of the frozen second in which they decide where all that speed goes.

Pillars
Pillar 1

Energy is time

Rino's arm is a clock of 12 steps. Every ability (dash, dash hop, wall run, wall jump, Loader) costs whole steps; running and the jump chain stay free.

Pillar 2

Freeze the moment

The Loader stops time on demand: hold to freeze your momentum in slow motion, load one to three steps, release to fly a straight line where you aim.

Pillar 3

The world winds you up

Steampunk machines move you for free and tick the clock forward. Capsules and broken machines refill it. Moves that cost steps never charge.

Pillar 4

A world you read at a glance

Brass helps you if you hit its rhythm, turquoise asks you to spend, overgrown nature tries to slow you down.

Scope & anti-scope

Attimo is not a combat game, not an open world and not a story game. It is one level of pure momentum, beaten by moving well.

In scope

  • Movement set: run, triple jump, dash, dash hop, wall run
  • Energy clock with 12 steps, 4 arm cores and Moto Perpetuo
  • Loader (right click): slow motion, 1–3 steps, straight-line flight
  • Extractor (left click) with its beam
  • Mechanism kit: 8 logic blocks, 3 themed layers
  • 1–2 simple enemy types (Extractor and Loader hits), health, checkpoints
  • One level, one boss, title to end screen
  • Final hero, steampunk world, juice, audio
  • Windows build, KB+M first, controller late

Nice to have

  • More looks per logic block (variants without new code)
  • More nature objects for the world layer
  • A clock face on the arm itself
  • Another hand module (the module system is dormant, ready for it)
  • Collectibles (hidden gears and cores)
  • Ledge grab

Out of scope

  • Multiple levels
  • Dialogue and cutscenes (story only through the world)
  • Shooting, weapons, a full combat system, many enemy types
  • Pull/push and the old aim mode
  • Skill trees, save slots, localization
  • Custom animation authoring (KayKit clips only)
  • Juice presets or toggles

Running and the jump chain are free; every ability costs time (steps).

Right click: the Loader paid in steps

  1. Hold: momentum freezes and is stored; Rino brakes hard or hangs in the air. Slow motion ~0.25×, free camera. The ring fills step by step (~0.3 s real time each), each step paid at once from the clock; max 3. Empty clock: no start (refusal). Capped at ~1.2 s real time, then it discharges on its own.
  2. Release: a straight line, no parabola, in the view direction (also up and down). Speed = own speed + push + steps × (push per step + k × own speed) (start: push 4, push per step 2 m/s, k 0.1, cap 22 m/s). Straight flight without gravity for 6 / 10 / 14 m by step count; speed above the cap lengthens it. Then gravity resumes and the speed carries on.
  3. Look: a smaller charge ring split into 3 step segments that light up as steps load. No trajectory line: the crosshair direction is the flight line. Slight desaturation and a turquoise vignette.
  4. Rules: on the ground Rino brakes hard and stands, the discharge may go up. One use per air phase, refilled on landing. Independent of the air dash; ~0.15 s air-control lock after the release. A Loader hit damages enemies (Phase 6).
  5. Camera: looks up almost vertically (~89°) everywhere, but never flips over the top to look backward.
  6. Feel: "geladen", a loaded spring. Holding builds tension (segments, rising hum, slight camera pull-back); the release is sharp with a short hitstop.
  7. Removed: goggle HUD, zoom, shoulder offset, aim pose, pull and push; the free redirect and the dashed trajectory preview.

Left click: the Extractor

Extractor hold

Cone in front of the hand (35 degrees, 4 m). Drains energy capsules (+3) and broken machines (+5); enemies in Phase 6. A turquoise beam runs from the hand to the source; the source goes dark and refills after its respawn time.

Shot removed

The shot, its charge ring and the gear-disc targets were removed on 2026-11-25. The module system stays in code but is dormant: the mouse wheel and keys 1/2 switch nothing until a later module arrives. Phase 6 enemies are beaten by the Extractor and Loader hits.

HUD (top left): the energy clock, Moto Perpetuo state and a momentum gauge; health joins it in Phase 6. The crosshair is drawn smaller while the Loader is held and inside the slingshot core. The gym area Zone_Energy is converted to steps in 05.1: the charge track goes, the shot stations become Loader-field obstacles without gates, and the move stations show their step costs.

Mechanism kit

Every world object has a profile on three axes: role (platform, environment, obstacle, booster, mechanism), energy relation (free, costs, amplified) and theme (steampunk, energy, nature). The cube is deliberately sparse; empty cells are intended.

Base ruleMeaning
CouplingSteampunk = free and mechanical, it helps you; never costs, but each booster use gives +1 step (gear spark, clock tick, small turquoise accent). Turquoise = energy; anything that costs or is amplified shows turquoise. Nature = world and danger, never helps you.
Platforms are neutralThey never cost, charge or get amplified; they only interact with the world and themselves (crumble, tilt, sink).
Booster ruleEvery ability has a free counterpart in the world, so a level section can dial how many steps it demands.

Slingshot core · the Loader you can touch

  1. Enter: a small brass cage catches Rino with a steam hiss only when he is inside it (no suction), momentum freezes.
  2. Hold: same slow motion and free camera as the Loader; a smaller pressure gauge instead of the charge ring, no trajectory line, smaller crosshair, auto-launch after the max hold.
  3. Release: steam blast, launch on the Loader's straight line at max(entry speed, fixed push), no steps paid, +1 step for using it.

Same input and timing as the Loader, so it teaches the Loader without a tutorial. It refills the Loader's air use and leaves the air dash alone.

Architecture · 8 logic blocks + 4 components + looks

Logic is separated from look. A block is one script with a .tres config; a component is a child node that adds an ability to any block; a look is an inherited scene. New objects are assembled, not coded.

Rules fixed in the Phase 05.1 discussions

  • Steam cannon: short fixed hold, then a launch along the barrel at a fixed speed, independent of the entry speed.
  • Steam wall: the nozzle push is gone; a steam-look wall pad runs and jumps exactly like the plain pad. Rino runs diagonally, head away from the wall.
  • Clock hand: a bounded back-and-forth sweep, always on, never steeper than the floor angle (no full turn; confirm in playtest).
  • Energy core: hold E to pay in, one step per tick; the amount stays, and each threshold (3 / 6 / 9 steps) switches one more output, never a door.
  • Loader field and attraction field: the field never breaks, a Loader with ≥ 2 steps glides through with a brake; walking out of the attraction field is free, a fast escape costs 1 step every 0.5 s.
  • Showroom = gym: one area per layer, grouped by role, plus a mixed course; the Phase 5 Loader room and energy area keep their layout, the shot stations become Loader-field obstacles.
