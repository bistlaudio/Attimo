Essential experience

Feel like a perpetual motion machine: every bit of speed charges your arm, and every charge throws you faster through an overgrown steampunk ruin, until you stop time, aim, and launch.

Attimo means "the moment". The player should leave with two feelings: the rush of never slowing down, and the calm control of the frozen second in which they decide where all that speed goes.

Pillars
Pillar 1

Momentum is power

Speed charges Rino's arm, and energy buys more speed. Every move feeds one loop: speed, energy, more speed.

Pillar 2

Freeze the moment

The Loader stops time on demand: hold to freeze your momentum in slow motion, look around, release to discharge it where you aim.

Pillar 3

Free base, charged power

Every move works without energy. Energy injects it: higher, longer, further. There is never a "failed" moment, only a stronger option.

Pillar 4

A world you read at a glance

Brass helps you if you hit its rhythm, turquoise asks you to spend, overgrown nature tries to slow you down.

Scope & anti-scope

Attimo is not a combat game, not an open world and not a story game. It is one level of pure momentum, beaten by moving well.

In scope

  • Movement set: run, triple jump, dash, dash hop, wall run
  • Energy arm with 4 cores and Moto Perpetuo
  • Loader (right click) with slow motion and trajectory
  • Extractor and Shot modules on the mouse wheel
  • Mechanism kit: 8 logic blocks, 3 themed layers
  • 1–2 simple enemy types, health, checkpoints
  • One level, one boss, title to end screen
  • Final hero, steampunk world, juice, audio
  • Windows build, KB+M first, controller late

Nice to have

  • More looks per logic block (variants without new code)
  • More nature objects for the world layer
  • Third hand module
  • Collectibles (hidden gears and cores)
  • Ledge grab

Out of scope

  • Multiple levels
  • Dialogue and cutscenes (story only through the world)
  • Full combat system, weapons, many enemy types
  • Pull/push and the old aim mode
  • Skill trees, save slots, localization
  • Custom animation authoring (KayKit clips only)
  • Juice presets or toggles

Extract vs. shoot is a real choice: the Extractor is more efficient (+35 vs. net +15) but slow and risky; shoot-and-absorb stays in the flow and is more fun.

Right click: the Loader replaces aim mode

  1. Hold: momentum freezes and is stored; Rino brakes hard or hangs in the air. Slow motion ~0.25×, free camera, capped at ~1.2 s real time, then it discharges on its own.
  2. Release: discharge in the view direction (also up and down) at max(stored speed, minimum push) + bonus, capped.
  3. Look: the crosshair becomes a charge ring that fills with the stored speed, a dashed trajectory preview, slight desaturation and a turquoise vignette. Empty arm: grey ring, no bonus segment.
  4. Rules: on the ground Rino brakes hard and stands, the discharge may go up. One use per air phase, refilled on landing. Independent of the air dash; ~0.15 s air-control lock after the release.
  5. As built: while holding, the camera pitch limit eases to about 75 degrees so a near-vertical discharge (super jump) works; the dashed preview is turquoise only with a full core. In the gym only a discharge with a full core clears the 7 m gap and the 5 m ledge.
  6. Feel: "geladen", a loaded spring. Holding builds tension (ring, rising hum, slight camera pull-back); the release is sharp with a short hitstop. The Loader ring turns the other way and looks clearly different from the shot charge ring.
  7. Removed: goggle HUD, zoom, shoulder offset, aim pose, pull and push. The small crosshair and target aim assist stay.

Left click: two modules on the mouse wheel

Extractor hold

Key 2. Cone in front of the hand (35 degrees, 4 m). Drains energy capsules and broken machines (enemies and shards in Phase 6). Brings energy; the source goes dark and refills after its respawn time.

Shot tap or hold

A flying projectile, never a beam (Mega Man / Samus). Key 1. Tap = 10 (reach 15 m); holding drains energy continuously while the shot grows, up to 25 (reach 25 m, the only shot that turns heavy mechanisms), with a clear full-charge tell (notched clockwise ring, palm flash, beep). Under 10 it does not fire; targets only glow as shootable while the arm can pay. Strong snap assist bends the shot to the locked target; targets hit in motion are gear discs. Targets hit from the hip replace levers.

HUD (top left): 4 core chambers, Moto Perpetuo state, active module and a momentum gauge; health joins it in Phase 6. Both modules and the HUD arrived in Phase 5 (Extractor on capsules and machines only). The mouse wheel and keys 1 and 2 only switch modules, there is no camera zoom. The gym area Zone_Energy behind the Loader room shows it all: a 114 m charge track, capsule, broken machine, charge spot, a drain plate and a capsule next to every injected move, and shot stations; every shot puzzle has a capsule at hand.

Slingshot core · the Loader you can touch

  1. Enter: a brass cage catches Rino with a steam hiss, momentum freezes.
  2. Hold: same slow motion and free camera as the Loader; a pressure gauge instead of the charge ring, brass trajectory dashes, auto-launch after the max hold.
  3. Release: steam blast, launch in the view direction at max(entry speed, fixed push), no energy bonus.

Same input and timing as the Loader, so it teaches the Loader without a tutorial. Entering starts the hold at once; releasing RMB (or a tap) fires, otherwise it auto-launches at the Loader cap. It refills the Loader's air use and leaves the air dash alone.

Architecture · 8 logic blocks + 4 components + looks

Logic is separated from look. A block is one script with a .tres config; a component is a child node that adds an ability to any block; a look is an inherited scene. New objects are assembled, not coded.

Parallels between layers come from components: a steampunk object plus amplification becomes its energy counterpart. A look may change freely within its layer but never breaks the coupling.

Rules fixed in the Phase 05.1 discuss

  • Steam cannon: short fixed hold, then a launch along the barrel at a fixed speed, independent of the entry speed.
  • Steam wall: the nozzle push is gone; a steam-look wall pad runs and jumps exactly like the plain pad, with Rino upright along the wall.
  • Clock hand: a bounded back-and-forth sweep, always on, never steeper than the floor angle (no full turn; confirm in playtest).
  • Targets: gear discs fired only by a shot, with a capsule nearby; a heavy gear disc needs a full-charge shot and replaces the valve.
  • Energy core: hold E to pay in; the amount stays, and each threshold (25 / 50 / 75) switches one more output.
  • Barrier and attraction field: only a Loader discharge with bonus breaks the barrier; walking out of the attraction field is free, a fast escape costs 20/s.
  • Showroom = gym: one area per layer, grouped by role, plus a mixed course; the Phase 5 Loader room and energy area keep their layout, with levers and the valve swapped in place for gear discs.
